## 摘要

Most explainable AI (XAI) frameworks are limited in their expressiveness, summarizing complex feature effects as single scalar values ($\phi_i$). This approach answers \emph{what} features are important but fails to reveal \emph{how} they interact. Furthermore, methods that attempt to capture interactions, like those based on Shapley values, often face an exponential computational cost. We present STRIDE, a scalable framework that addresses both limitations by reframing explanation as a subset-enumeration-free, orthogonal \textbf{functional decomposition} in a Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space
