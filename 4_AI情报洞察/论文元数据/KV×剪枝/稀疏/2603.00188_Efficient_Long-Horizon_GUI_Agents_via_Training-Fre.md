## 摘要

Large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have emerged as powerful engines for autonomous GUI agents, yet their deployment is severely constrained by the substantial memory footprint and latency of the Key-Value (KV) cache during long-horizon interactions. While existing cache compression methods have proven effective for LLMs, we empirically demonstrate that they suffer from suboptimal performance in GUI scenarios due to a fundamental misalignment: unlike general visual tasks where attention sparsity varies across layers, GUI attention patterns exhibit uniform high-sparsity across all transformer l
