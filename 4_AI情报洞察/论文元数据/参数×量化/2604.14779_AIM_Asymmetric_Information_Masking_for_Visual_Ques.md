## 摘要

In continual visual question answering (VQA), existing Continual Learning (CL) methods are mostly built for symmetric, unimodal architectures. However, modern Vision-Language Models (VLMs) violate this assumption, as their trainable components are inherently asymmetric. This structural mismatch renders VLMs highly prone to catastrophic forgetting when learning from continuous data streams. Specifically, the asymmetry causes standard global regularization to favor the massive language decoder during optimization, leaving the smaller but critical visual projection layers highly vulnerable to int
