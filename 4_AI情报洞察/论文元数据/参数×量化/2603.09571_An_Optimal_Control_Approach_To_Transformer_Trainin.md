## 摘要

In this paper, we develop a rigorous optimal control-theoretic approach to Transformer training that respects key structural constraints such as (i) realized-input-independence during execution, (ii) the ensemble control nature of the problem, and (iii) positional dependence. We model the Transformer architecture as a discrete-time controlled particle system with shared actions, exhibiting noise-free McKean-Vlasov dynamics. While the resulting dynamics is not Markovian, we show that lifting it to probability measures produces a fully-observed Markov decision process (MDP). Positional encodings
