Classification of  electroencephalogram (EEG) and electrocorticogram (ECoG) signals obtained during motor-imagery (MI) has substantial application potential, including for communication assistance and rehabilitation support for patients with motor impairments. These signals remain inherently susceptible to physiological artifacts (e.g., eye blinking, swallowing), which pose persistent challenges.
