## 摘要

Standard Transformers impose near-exponential decay on the influence of distant tokens, conflicting with the power-law structure of long-range dependencies in natural language. We introduce the \emph{Variable-Order Retention Transformer} (\VORT{}), a memory architecture in which each ingested token is assigned a learnable fractional order α_i\in[δ,1] that governs a Grünwald--Letnikov power-law retention kernel.
