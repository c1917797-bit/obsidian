## 摘要

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) relies on large-scale Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search (ANNS) to retrieve semantically relevant context for large language models. Among ANNS methods, IVF-PQ offers an attractive balance between memory efficiency and search accuracy. However, achieving high recall requires reranking which fetches full-precision vectors for reranking, and the billion-scale vector databases need to reside in CPU DRAM or SSD due to the limited capacity of GPU HBM.
