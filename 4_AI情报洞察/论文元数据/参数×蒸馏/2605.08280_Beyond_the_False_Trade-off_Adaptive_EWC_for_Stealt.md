## 摘要

Preserving model fidelity is essential for stealthy text-to-image (T2I) backdoor attacks. Existing methods such as Learning without Forgetting (LwF) rely on output-based distillation, which provides limited regularization. We introduce Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) as a parameter-based alternative for preserving fidelity in backdoor learning.
