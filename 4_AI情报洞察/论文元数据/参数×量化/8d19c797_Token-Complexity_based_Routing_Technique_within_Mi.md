## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures have emerged as a powerful technique for improving and scaling Large Language Models by conditionally activating Feed Forward subnetworks and distributing tokens through a routing system,  within the Transformer layers. However, existing MoE methods often rely on static top-k routing strategies that do not involve token-level variability in complexity, leading to suboptimal expert utilization. In this research, we propose a novel token-complexity-based routing framework that dynamically allocates tokens to either lightweight or strong feedforward networks
