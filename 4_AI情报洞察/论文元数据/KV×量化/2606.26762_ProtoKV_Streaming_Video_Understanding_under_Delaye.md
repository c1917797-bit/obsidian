## 摘要

Streaming video understanding (SVU) must answer queries that arrive asynchronously while visual tokens stream continuously under strict GPU-memory and query-time latency budgets. A key challenge is delayed query: decisive cues may appear briefly, yet many subsequent updates occur before the query arrives, increasing the risk that those cues are evicted or diluted under bounded memory. We propose ProtoKV, a constant-footprint SVU memory that represents far history as a fixed-capacity summary state rather than retaining token instances.
