## 摘要

State space model (SSM) have emerged as a strong alternative to transformer owing to its linear-time complexity and state retention mechanism where the computation efficiency and memory capability are enhanced especially in long-sequence tasks. However, the features derived from state updates in SSM still exhibit weaker representation than those generated by self-attention in transformer.
