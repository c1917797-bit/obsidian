## 摘要

Post-training quantization (PTQ) has become an important technique for reducing the inference cost of Large Language Models (LLMs). While recent mixed-precision methods improve ultra-low bit quantization by preserving critical subspaces in high precision, they typically construct these subspaces relying solely on activation statistics. This ignores the fundamental nature of linear operations, where the output perturbation is jointly driven by both activation and weight quantization noise.
