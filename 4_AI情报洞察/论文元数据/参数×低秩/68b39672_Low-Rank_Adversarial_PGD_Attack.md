## 摘要

Adversarial attacks on deep neural networks have become essential tools for studying model robustness, with Projected Gradient Descent (PGD) being widely adopted due to its effectiveness and computational efficiency. In this work, we provide empirical evidence that PGD perturbations are, on average, low-rank, with their magnitude concentrated in the bottom part of the singular value spectrum across CIFAR-10 and ImageNet datasets and multiple architectures. Building on this insight, we introduce LoRa-PGD, a simple low-rank variation of PGD that directly computes adversarial attacks with control
