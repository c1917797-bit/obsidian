## 摘要

Graph neural networks (GNNs) tightly couple their input-output parameters to dataset-specific feature spaces and target sets, exhibiting limited transferability across different datasets. In contrast, language models (LMs) generalize flexibly via a unified input-output interface, motivating recent attempts to adapt LMs to graph tasks. However, existing methods struggle to encode whole-graph information, leading to potential information loss and suboptimal graph understanding.
