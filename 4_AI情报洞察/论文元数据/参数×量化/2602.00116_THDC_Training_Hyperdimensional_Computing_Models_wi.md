## 摘要

Hyperdimensional computing (HDC) offers lightweight learning for energy-constrained devices by encoding data into high-dimensional vectors. However, its reliance on ultra-high dimensionality and static, randomly initialized hypervectors limits memory efficiency and learning capacity. Therefore, we propose Trainable Hyperdimensional Computing (THDC), which enables end-to-end HDC via backpropagation. THDC replaces randomly initialized vectors with trainable embeddings and introduces a one-layer binary neural network to optimize class representations. Evaluated on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-1
