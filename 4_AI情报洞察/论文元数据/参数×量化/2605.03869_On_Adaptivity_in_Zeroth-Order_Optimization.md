## 摘要

We investigate the effectiveness of adaptive zeroth-order (ZO) optimization for memory-constrained fine-tuning of large language models (LLMs). Contrary to prior claims, we show that adaptive ZO methods such as ZO-Adam offer no convergence advantage over well-tuned ZO-SGD, while incurring significant memory overhead. Our analysis reveals that in high dimensions, ZO gradients lack coordinate-wise heterogeneity, rendering adaptive mechanisms memory inefficient.
