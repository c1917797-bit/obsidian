## 摘要

Key-value (KV) caching accelerates inference of large language models (LLMs) by reusing past computations for generated tokens. Its importance becomes even greater in long-context applications such as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and in-context learning (ICL). However, conventional KV caching embeds positional information directly into the cache, limiting its reusability.
