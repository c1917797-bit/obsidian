## 摘要

The sparse and discrete nature of point clouds fundamentally limits their effectiveness in perception tasks, as these raw 3D data collections inadequately capture the continuous geometry and detailed appearance of complex real-world scenes. We propose **GCept**, a unified 3D perception framework that evolves raw points into adaptive Gaussian primitives, representing a natural progression in point cloud enrichment.
