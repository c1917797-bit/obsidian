## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) can struggle to memorize factual knowledge in their parameters, often leading to hallucinations and poor performance on knowledge-intensive tasks. In this paper, we formalize fact memorization from an information-theoretic perspective and study how training data distributions affect fact accuracy. We show that fact accuracy is suboptimal (below the capacity limit) whenever the amount of information contained in the training data facts exceeds model capacity.
