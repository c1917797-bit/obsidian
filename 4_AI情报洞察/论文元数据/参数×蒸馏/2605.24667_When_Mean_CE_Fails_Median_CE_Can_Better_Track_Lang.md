## 摘要

Mean cross-entropy is the standard validation metric for language models, but it can fail to track model quality during training. We examine this in two common scenarios. First, in Qwen2.5-1.5B SFT on synthetic fact-learning, we find that mean CE rises substantially after the initial learning phase while held-out fact-recall accuracy remains near its peak.
