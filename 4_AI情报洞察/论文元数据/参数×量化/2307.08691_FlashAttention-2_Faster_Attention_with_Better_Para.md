## 摘要

Scaling Transformers to longer sequences has been limited by the quadratic complexity and memory footprint of attention. FlashAttention-2 is an algorithm that computes attention exactly while being faster and more memory-efficient than standard attention. We make FlashAttention better by (1) better parallelism - we can now parallelize over the sequence length for decoding, (2) better work partitioning - we reduce the number of passes over the attention matrix, and (3) hardware-aware parallelism across heads. FlashAttention-2 is 1.6x faster than FlashAttention and enables training of models 2x the size on the same hardware.
