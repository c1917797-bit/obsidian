## 摘要

Accurate delineation of kidney tumours in Computed Tomography (CT) is essential for downstream quantitative analysis and precision oncology, but manual segmentation is a specialised task, time-consuming and difficult to scale. Automated 3D segmentation remains challenging because CT scans are large volumetric images, making high-resolution dense convolutional networks computationally expensive and often dependent on downsampling or patch-based inference. We propose a two-stage 3D segmentation methodology based on voxel sparsification and submanifold sparse convolutional networks (SSCNs). Stage
