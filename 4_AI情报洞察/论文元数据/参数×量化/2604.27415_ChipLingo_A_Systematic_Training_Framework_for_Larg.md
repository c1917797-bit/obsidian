## 摘要

With the rapid advancement of semiconductor technology, Electronic Design Automation (EDA) has become an increasingly knowledge-intensive and document-driven engineering domain. Although large language models (LLMs) have shown strong general capabilities, applying them directly to EDA remains challenging due to limited domain expertise, cross-tool knowledge confusion, and degraded retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) performance after domain training. To address these issues, this paper presents ChipLingo, a systematic training pipeline for domain-adapted LLMs tailored to EDA scenarios.   Chip
