## 摘要

Driver fatigue is a critical safety concern in advanced driver assistance systems. Driver monitoring models trained off-site on static datasets adapt poorly to real-world conditions, while standard federated learning imposes high communication overhead, assumes homogeneous architectures, and struggles with personalized driver data. We present FedADAS, a federated distillation framework enabling collaborative on-device learning across heterogeneous vehicular networks.
