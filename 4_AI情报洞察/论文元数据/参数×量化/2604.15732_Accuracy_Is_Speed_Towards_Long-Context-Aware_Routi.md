## 摘要

Distributed LLM serving systems optimize per-request latency and throughput. However, under long-context workloads, inference accuracy becomes more variable. When incorrect responses trigger retries, accuracy directly translates into cumulative user-visible delay that is not captured by single-shot latency metrics.   In this work, we argue that under long-context serving, \textbf{accuracy becomes speed} through retry dynamics.
