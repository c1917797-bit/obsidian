## 摘要

The massive vocabulary sizes of large language models, often exceeding 100k tokens, impose a computational bottleneck on the final linear projection layer during speculative decoding. Existing vocabulary pruning solutions rely on static or coarsely-grained sub-vocabularies that necessitate large active sizes ($\sim$30k) to maintain draft quality. We propose NanoSpec, a novel training-free approach that breaks this trade-off by dynamically constructing a minimalist, context-aware active vocabulary for each generation step.
