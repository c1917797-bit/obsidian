## 摘要

Neural network compression is commonly achieved by pruning parameters based on local importance scores, e.g., magnitude-based pruning. We propose a complementary approach that compresses models by aggregating neurons with similar functional behavior rather than removing weights independently. Our method encodes a trained network as a polynomial ODE system and applies a lumping method called Approximate Forward Differential Equivalence to identify neurons with approximately matching induced dynamics.
