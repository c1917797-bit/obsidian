## 摘要

Fine-Grained Mixture of Experts is a powerful architecture for scaling large models, yet its application in specialized domains like medicine remains underexplored. In this work, we conduct the first systematic study of expert granularity in a medical multimodal VQA context. Our findings reveal a fundamental trade-off: while increasing granularity significantly enhances out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization and robustness, it slightly degrades in-distribution (ID) fitting and, notably, amplifies expert co-occurrence and functional similarity, indicating stronger collaborative tendencies amo
