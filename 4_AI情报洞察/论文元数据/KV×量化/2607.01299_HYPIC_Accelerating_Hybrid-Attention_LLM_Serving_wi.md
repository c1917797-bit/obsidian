## 摘要

In retrieval augmented generation (RAG) and agentic LLM serving, prompts are assembled from independent segments into long contexts, making the prefill stage dominate the per-request computation cost. To this cost, two directions have emerged in parallel: position-independent caching (PIC) admits KV reuse for non-contiguous segments shared across different requests, while hybrid-attention models reduce computation complexity by replacing most full-attention layers with linear attention.
