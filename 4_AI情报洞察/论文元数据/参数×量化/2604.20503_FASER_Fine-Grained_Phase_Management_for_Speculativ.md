## 摘要

Speculative decoding (SD) is a widely used approach for accelerating decode-heavy LLM inference workloads. While online inference workloads are highly dynamic, existing SD systems are rigid and take a coarse-grained approach to SD management. They typically set the speculative token length for an entire batch and serialize the execution of the draft and verification phases. Consequently, these systems fall short at adapting to volatile online inference traffic. Under low load, they exhibit prolonged latency because the draft phase blocks the verification phase for the entire batch, leaving GPU
