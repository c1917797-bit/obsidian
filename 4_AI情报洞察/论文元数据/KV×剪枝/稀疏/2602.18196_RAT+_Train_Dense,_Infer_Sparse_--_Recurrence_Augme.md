## 摘要

Structured dilated attention has an appealing inference-time efficiency knob: it reduces the FLOPs of attention and the KV cache size by a factor of the dilation size D, while preserving long-range connectivity. While prior work studies it by training each configuration from scratch, directly sparsifying a pretrained attention model into a dilated pattern leads to severe accuracy degradation, preventing flexible reuse across inference scenarios.
