This paper revisits multi-play multi-armed bandit with shareable arm capacities problem (MP-MAB-SAC), for the purpose of 
revealing fundamental insights on the statistical limits and data efficient learning. The MP-MAB-SAC is tailored for resource allocation problems arising from LLM inference serving, edge intelligence, etc. It consists of $K$ arms and each arm $k$ is associated with an unknown but deterministic capacity $m_k$ and per-unit capacity reward with mean $\mu_k$ and $\sigma$ sub-Gaus
