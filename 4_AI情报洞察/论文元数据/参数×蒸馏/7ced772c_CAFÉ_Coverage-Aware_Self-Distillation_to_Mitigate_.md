## 摘要

Deep neural networks rarely exhibit global overfitting in the classical sense, yet they often suffer from a less visible problem - forgetting of previously learned patterns. This phenomenon, which was termed local overfitting, degrades performance in specific regions of the input space even as overall accuracy improves. To address this problem, we propose CAFÉ (Coverage-Aware Forgetting Elimination) - an online, validation-aware, single model method, which mitigates forgetting during training while exploiting self-distillation.
