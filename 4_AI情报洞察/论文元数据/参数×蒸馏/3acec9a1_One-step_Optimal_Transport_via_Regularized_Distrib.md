## 摘要

Unpaired domain translation remains a challenging task due to the need of finding a balance between faithfulness and realism. Diffusion-based methods for unpaired translation typically excel at realism, but require numerous inference steps and tend to offer suboptimal input-output alignment. Many of the optimal transport (OT) based methods, on the other hand, offer efficient few-step inference and reach superior input-output alignment, but heavily rely on adversarial training and inherit its shortcomings. In this paper, we propose a method called Regularized Distribution Matching Distillation
