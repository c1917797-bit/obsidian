## 摘要

Unstructured Electronic Health Record (EHR) data, such as clinical notes, contain clinical contextual observations that are not directly reflected in structured data fields. This additional information can substantially improve model learning. However, due to their unstructured nature, these data are often unavailable or impractical to use when deploying a model. We introduce a multimodal learning framework that leverages unstructured EHR data during training while producing a model that can be deployed using only structured EHR data.
