## 摘要

The verbose context problem occurs when structured concepts have token-inefficient textual representations. This bottleneck is acute in population health: cohort-level analysis of longitudinal patient records requires reasoning over thousands of medically-coded events, often exceeding 400K tokens in total. We present PopMedQA, a benchmark isolating this problem through computational tasks on groups of longitudinal patient records.
