## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and looped architectures scale models along two orthogonal axes, namely parameter capacity and effective depth. However, mainstream looped architectures rely on dense backbones that couple parameter count with per-token FLOPs, which makes it impossible to isolate the effect of iterative computation under matched budgets.
