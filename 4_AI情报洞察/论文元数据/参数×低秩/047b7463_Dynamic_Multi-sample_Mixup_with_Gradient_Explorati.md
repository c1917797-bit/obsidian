## 摘要

This paper studies the problem of open-set graph anomaly detection, which aims to generalize a graph neural network (GNN) trained with a small number of both normal and abnormal nodes to detect unseen anomalies different from training anomalies during inference. This problem is highly challenging due to both the data scarcity of unseen anomalies and the label scarcity for training nodes.
