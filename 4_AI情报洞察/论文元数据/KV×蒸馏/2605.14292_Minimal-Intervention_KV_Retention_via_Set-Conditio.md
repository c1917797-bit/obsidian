## 摘要

We study seven mechanisms across these five families under matched mean cache on long-form mathematical reasoning (MATH-500~\cite{hendrycks2021math}) with two distilled-reasoning models (Qwen-7B and Llama-8B variants of DeepSeek-R1-Distill~\cite{deepseek2025r1}) at budgets $b \in \{64, 128\}$.
