## 摘要

SAKURAONE is a managed high performance computing (HPC) cluster developed and operated by the SAKURA Internet Research Center. It builds on the KOKARYOKU PHY bare metal GPU platform and is optimized for advanced workloads, including large language model (LLM) training. In ISC 2025 TOP500, SAKURAONE is ranked 49th by HPL and is the only top 100 system that uses a fully open networking stack - 800 GbE with SONiC - demonstrating the scalability of vendor-neutral technology.
