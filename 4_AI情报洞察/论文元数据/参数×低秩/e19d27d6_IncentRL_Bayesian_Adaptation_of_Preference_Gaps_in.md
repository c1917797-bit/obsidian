## 摘要

Reinforcement learning agents often struggle in sparse-reward settings, where intrinsic signals such as curiosity or empowerment are used to aid exploration. Existing approaches typically rely on fixed trade-offs between extrinsic and intrinsic rewards, limiting adaptability across tasks. We introduce IncentRL, a cognitively inspired framework that unifies external rewards with internal preferences through adaptive incentive shaping.
