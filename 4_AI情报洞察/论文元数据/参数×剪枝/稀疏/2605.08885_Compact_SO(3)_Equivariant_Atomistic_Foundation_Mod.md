## 摘要

SO(3) equivariant graph neural networks have become the dominant paradigm for atomistic foundation models, achieving high accuracy and data efficiency by building rotational symmetry directly into the architecture. Yet the computational cost of their higher-order tensor operations creates a tough trade-off between model accuracy and inference efficiency. In this paper, we propose a structural pruning method for SO(3) equivariant atomistic foundation models to bridge this accuracy-efficiency gap.
