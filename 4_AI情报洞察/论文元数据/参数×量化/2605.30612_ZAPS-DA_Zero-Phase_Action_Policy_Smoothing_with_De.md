## 摘要

Continuous control policies trained with off-policy reinforcement learning frequently exhibit high-frequency action jitter, rendering direct deployment on physical actuators impractical. Post-hoc filtering attenuates jitter but introduces phase lag; embedding smoothness penalties in the actor's loss couples them with the RL gradient and conflates reward regression with over-aggressive smoothing. We present ZAPS-DA, a framework that reduces action jitter at deployment with negligible phase lag and no post-processing.
