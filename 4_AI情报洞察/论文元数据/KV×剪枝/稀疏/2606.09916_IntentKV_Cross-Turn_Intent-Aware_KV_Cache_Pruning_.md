## 摘要

Multi-turn LLM agents fan short queries into long trajectories of tool calls, search results, and intermediate reasoning. Both KV memory and KV read bandwidth grow by orders of magnitude across a single trajectory, making the key-value (KV) cache, not parameter compute, the dominant serving bottleneck for long-horizon agents.
