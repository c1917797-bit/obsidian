## 摘要

Video Virtual Try-On (VVT) aims to synthesize garments that appear natural across consecutive video frames, capturing both their dynamics and interactions with human motion. Despite recent progress, existing VVT methods still suffer from inadequate garment fidelity and limited spatiotemporal consistency. The reasons are (i) under-exploitation of garment information, with limited garment cues being injected, resulting in weaker fine-detail fidelity, and (ii) the lack of spatiotemporal modeling, which hampers cross-frame identity consistency and causes temporal jitter and appearance drift.
