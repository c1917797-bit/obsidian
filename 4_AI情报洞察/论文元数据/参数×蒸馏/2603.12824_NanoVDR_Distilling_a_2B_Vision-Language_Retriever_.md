## 摘要

Vision-Language Model (VLM) based retrievers have advanced visual document retrieval (VDR) to impressive quality. They require the same multi-billion parameter encoder for both document indexing and query encoding, incurring high latency and GPU dependence even for plain-text queries. We observe that this design is unnecessarily symmetric: documents are visually complex and demand strong visual understanding, whereas queries are just short text strings.
