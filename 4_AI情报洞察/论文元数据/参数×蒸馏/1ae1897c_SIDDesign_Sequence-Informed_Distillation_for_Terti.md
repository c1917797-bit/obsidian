## 摘要

Tertiary structure-based RNA design, which aims to design nucleotide sequences that fold into a given 3D structure, is a fundamental challenge in synthetic biology and structure-guided design. Although recent work has advanced geometric encoders and architectural innovations, progress remains constrained by the scarcity and bias of resolved RNA tertiary structures. Considering that RNA folding and design are approximate inverse tasks, and that RNA foundation models employed in folding models capture rich RNA priors from large-scale sequence data, we are inspired to exploit these representation
