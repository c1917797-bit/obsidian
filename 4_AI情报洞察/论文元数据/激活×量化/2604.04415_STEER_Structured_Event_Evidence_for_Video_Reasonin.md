## 摘要

Human understanding of video dynamics relies on forming structured representations of entities, actions, and temporal relations before engaging in abstract reasoning. In contrast, existing Video-LLMs apply unstructured chain-of-thought directly to raw visual tokens, where critical temporal cues are buried in verbose narration and event-level structure is largely overlooked. We propose Structured Event Evidence, which represents a video as a compact, time-ordered event schema capturing salient events with key attributes and inter-event temporal dependencies, enabling evidence-grounded reasoning
