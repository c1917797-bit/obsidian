## 摘要

We propose a novel graph-guided sparse learning model using $\ell_0$ norm. The proposed model addresses a key limitation of existing methods, which enforce neighboring variables to have similar coefficients. We introduce a novel relaxation for the proposed problem. Our approach is based on reformulating the original model exactly as a Boolean convex program. We analyze the first-order relaxation and derive the necessary and sufficient conditions for exactness.
