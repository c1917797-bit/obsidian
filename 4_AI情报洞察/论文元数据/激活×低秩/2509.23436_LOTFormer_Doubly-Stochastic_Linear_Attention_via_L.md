## 摘要

Transformers have proven highly effective across modalities, but standard softmax attention scales quadratically with sequence length, limiting long context modeling. Linear attention mitigates this by approximating attention with kernel feature maps, yet most attention mechanisms remain row normalized and can over concentrate mass on a few tokens, harming robustness and information flow.
