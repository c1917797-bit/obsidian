## 摘要

Current image editing software often hinges on fixed filters or expert tuning, leaving a gap between amateur users' intent and outcomes. Creations by generative models may contain artifacts, implausible details, or stylistic drift away from photorealism and offer little insight into why an edit was made. We propose IEA, a conversational Image Editing Agent that learns to operate parameterized tools in an explicit, interpretable action space.
