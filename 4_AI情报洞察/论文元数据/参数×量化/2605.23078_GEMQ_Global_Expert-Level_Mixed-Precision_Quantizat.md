## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts Large Language Models (MoE-LLMs) achieve strong performance but incur substantial memory overhead due to massive expert parameters. Mixed-precision quantization mitigates this cost by allocating expert-wise bit-widths based on their importance, approaching the accuracy-memory Pareto frontier and enabling extreme low-bit quantization.
