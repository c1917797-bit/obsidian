## 摘要

**Steady-state visual evoked potentials (SSVEPs)** provide a high-throughput testbed for neural decoding, yet real-world deployment is hindered by *subject-specific calibration*. We address this challenge by proposing **DATCAN**, a framework that *embeds domain-specific invariances into contrastive learning* while *aligning feature statistics without supervision*. DATCAN integrates three complementary components: (i) a **harmonic-aware contrastive objective** that encodes *frequency-locked physiological priors* directly into the embedding space, (ii) **second-order covariance alignment (CORAL)
