## 摘要

Compositional generalization requires models to produce novel configurations from familiar parts. In diffusion models, prior compositional generation methods typically assume that the relevant concepts or conditioning signals are already available. We instead ask whether a pretrained diffusion model can discover query-specific concepts from the time-indexed scores it learns for the noisy marginals $p_t(x_t)$ and compose them at test time. Given a single out-of-distribution query, our method performs gradient ascent on $s_θ(x_t,t) \approx \nabla_{x_t}\log p_t(x_t)$ at multiple noising timesteps
