## 摘要

We investigate strategies for adapting small, efficient language models to Faroese, a low-resource North Germanic language. Starting from English-pretrained models, we apply continued pre-training on related Scandinavian languages -- individually or combined via model merging -- before fine-tuning on Faroese. We compare full fine-tuning with parameter-efficient adaptation via LoRA, assessing their effects on general language modeling performance, linguistic accuracy, and text comprehension.
