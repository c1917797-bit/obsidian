## 摘要

Learning matrix-valued distributions from high-dimensional and possibly incomplete training data is challenging: ambient-space generative modeling is computationally expensive and statistically fragile when the matrix dimension is large but the sample size is limited. We propose CoreFlow, a geometry-preserving low-rank flow model that learns shared row/column subspaces across the matrix distribution, and then trains a continuous normalizing flow only on the induced low-dimensional core. CoreFlow is designed for settings where shared low-rank matrix geometry is present, especially in high-dimen
