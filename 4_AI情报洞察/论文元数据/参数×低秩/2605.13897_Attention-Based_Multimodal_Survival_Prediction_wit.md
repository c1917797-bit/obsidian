## 摘要

We propose a novel multimodal deep learning framework for patient-level survival prediction, which integrates whole-slide histology features, RNA-seq expression profiles, and clinical variables. Our architecture combines an ABMIL module~\cite{ilse2018attention} for slide-level representation with feedforward encoders for RNA and clinical data. These embeddings are then integrated through low-rank bilinear cross-modal fusion~\cite{liu2018efficient} to model conditional interactions across modalities while controlling parameter growth.
