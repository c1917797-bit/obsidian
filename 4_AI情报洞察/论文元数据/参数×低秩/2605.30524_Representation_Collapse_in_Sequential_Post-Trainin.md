## 摘要

Large language models are now adapted through chains of post-training stages rather than through a single instruction-tuning pass. This paper studies whether such sequential post-training gradually compresses internal representations into low-rank, anisotropic, and homogeneous feature spaces. We define a measurement suite for hidden states, logits, token trajectories, and LoRA updates, and we use it to analyze supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, safety/refusal tuning, math and code specialization, and long chain-of-thought tuning under controlled stage orderings.
