## 摘要

Semi-structured N:M sparsity and low-bit quantization (e.g., 1.58-bit BitNet) are two promising approaches for improving the efficiency of large language models (LLMs), yet they have largely been studied in isolation. In this work, we investigate their interaction and show that 1.58-bit BitNet is naturally more compatible with N:M sparsity than full-precision models. To study this effect, we propose Sparse-BitNet, a unified framework that jointly applies 1.58-bit quantization and dynamic N:M sparsification while ensuring stable training for the first time.
