## 摘要

We propose a parameter-efficient fine-tuning method for large language models. We froze pre-trained model weights and train only a small number of bonus matrices with significantly reduced trainable parameters. When applied to dense layers, our low-rank bonus updates have a theoretical connection to principal component analysis, which reveals that training only bonus matrices with low-rank decomposition can be considered as learning a most important residual from pre-trained model.
