This paper addresses the challenge of answering technical questions derived from Japan's River and Sediment Control Technical Standards -- a multi-volume regulatory document covering survey, planning, design, and maintenance of river levees, dams, and sabo structures -- using open-source large language models running entirely on local hardware.
