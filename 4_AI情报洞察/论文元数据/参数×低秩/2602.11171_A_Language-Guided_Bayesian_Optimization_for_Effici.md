## 摘要

Fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) offers a resource-efficient way to personalize or specialize. However, LoRA is highly sensitive to hyperparameter choices, and exhaustive hyperparameter search is computationally expensive. To address this, we propose a Bayesian Optimization (BO) framework that leverages the domain knowledge of pre-trained LLMs to efficiently search for LoRA hyperparameters. Our approach repurposes a pre-trained LLM as a discrete-to-continuous mapping module to link hyperparameters and their domain knowledge to a continuous vector space,
