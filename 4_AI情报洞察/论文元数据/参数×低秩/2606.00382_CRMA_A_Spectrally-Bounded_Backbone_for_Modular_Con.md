## 摘要

Sequential fine-tuning of large language models forces a choice: let the shared substrate keep learning and accept catastrophic forgetting, or freeze it after task one and foreclose cross-task refinement. Per-task adapter methods (LoRAHub, AdapterFusion, PackNet, Progressive Networks) take the second path. We introduce CRMA (Constrained Residual Mixing Adapter), a residual adapter whose internal mixing matrix M is doubly-stochastic at every forward pass via Sinkhorn normalization, so by Birkhoff's theorem ||M||_2 &lt;= 1 holds by construction -- a structural bound, not a penalty.
