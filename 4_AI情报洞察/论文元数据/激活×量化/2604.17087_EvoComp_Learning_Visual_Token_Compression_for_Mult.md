## 摘要

Recent Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have demonstrated strong performance on vision-language understanding tasks, yet their inference efficiency is often hampered by the large number of visual tokens, particularly in high-resolution or multi-image scenarios. To address this issue, we propose EvoComp, a visual token compression framework that significantly reduces token count while preserving task accuracy. EvoComp introduces a lightweight encoder-only transformer-based compressor that selects the most informative and non-redundant visual tokens by jointly considering visual and text
