## 摘要

On-Device deployment of large and small language models (LLMs / SLMs) faces critical challenges in balancing performance, energy consumption, and carbon footprint on various mobile and wearable devices. We introduce a hierarchical multi-objective reinforcement learning approach for dynamic Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) scaling that optimizes carbon efficiency as the primary objective while maintaining acceptable performance and energy consumption. Our method employs Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with a carbon-first reward function that prioritizes carbon efficiency (inferences per mg CO$_2$)
