## 摘要

Dataset Distillation (DD) compresses large-scale datasets into smaller synthesized datasets, enabling efficient model training while preserving high test performance. However, existing DD methods primarily focus on accuracy and largely neglect adversarial robustness, potentially exposing models to security risks in critical applications. Evaluating robustness is therefore essential but remains challenging due to complex interactions among distillation methods, model architectures, and attack strategies.
