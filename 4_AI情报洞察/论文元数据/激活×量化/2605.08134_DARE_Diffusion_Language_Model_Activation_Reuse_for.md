## 摘要

Diffusion Large Language Models (dLLMs) have emerged as a promising alternative to auto-regressive (AR) models, offering greater expressive capacity and potential for parallel generation and faster inference. However, open-source dLLMs remain immature, lagging behind AR models in both efficiency and quality.   We identify an underexplored property of dLLMs: *token-wise redundancy* in bi-directional self-attention.
