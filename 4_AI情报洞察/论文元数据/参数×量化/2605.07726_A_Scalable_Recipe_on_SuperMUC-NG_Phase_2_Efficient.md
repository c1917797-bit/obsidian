## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) continue to demonstrate superior performance with increasing scale, yet training models with billions to trillions of parameters requires staggering computational resources, e.g. a one-trillion-parameter GPT-style model requires an estimated 120 million exaflops. This challenge necessitates efficient distributed training strategies on cutting-edge High-Performance Computing (HPC) infrastructure. In this work, we explore the SuperMUC-NG Phase 2 (SMNG-P2) system at the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre (LRZ) in Garching, Germany, equipped with Intel Data Center GPU Max 1
