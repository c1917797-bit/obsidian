SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) systems depend on front-end components for feature detection and matching. Traditional methods use handcrafted or learning-based features, but both have limitations in robustness and efficiency. We propose a new SLAM front-end framework that fuses recent State Space Models (SSMs), specifically the Mamba architecture, with transformer-based attention.
