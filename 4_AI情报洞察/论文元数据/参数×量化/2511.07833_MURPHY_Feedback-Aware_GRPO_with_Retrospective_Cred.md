## 摘要

Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has become a standard recipe for post-training LLMs on reasoning tasks, with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) emerging as a leading approach. However, GRPO and its variants are inherently single-turn: they optimize from terminal rewards on isolated prompt-response pairs, leaving them poorly suited to agentic settings where models must iteratively refine solutions in response to environmental feedback.
