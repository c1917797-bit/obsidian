Bipartite knowledge graphs in niche domains are typically data-poor and edge-sparse, which hinders link prediction. We introduce AEGIS (Authentic Edge Growth In Sparsity), an edge-only augmentation framework that resamples existing training edges—either uniformly simple or with inverse-degree bias degree\_aware—thereby preserving the original node set and sidestepping fabricated endpoints.
