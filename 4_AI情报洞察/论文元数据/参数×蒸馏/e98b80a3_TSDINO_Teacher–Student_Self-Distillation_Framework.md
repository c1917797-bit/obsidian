## 摘要

Building time-series foundation models (TSFM) poses challenges in terms of learning stability due to limited data availability and heterogeneous temporal dynamics across various time-series datasets. We propose TSDINO, a teacher-student framework for robust pre-training of TSFM based on the principle of self-distillation with no labels. TSDINO offers a model-agnostic approach that combines two complementary objectives: (i) feature preservation under augmentations and (ii) masked patch prediction.
