## 摘要

Federated recommendation (FR) has emerged as a promising paradigm to enhance user privacy by training models in a distributed manner, where participants share model updates instead of raw data. Despite its advantages, three major challenges remain: (i) Privacy leakage. Direct parameter sharing risks exposing model information. (ii) Degraded performance. Parameter-transfer based methods often underperforms compared to centralized training due to limited knowledge exchange. (iii) Communication overhead.
