## 摘要

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques, particularly Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), have become essential for adapting Large Language Models (LLMs) to downstream tasks. While the recent FlyLoRA framework successfully leverages bio-inspired sparse random projections to mitigate parameter interference, it relies on a static, magnitude-based routing mechanism that is agnostic to input context. In this paper, we propose NeuroLoRA, a novel Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) based LoRA framework inspired by biological neuromodulation -- the dynamic regulation of neuronal excitability based on context.
