## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) are typically scaled through billions of parameters and trillions of tokens, making progress largely restricted to organizations with substantial resources. Recent work on Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) upcycling shows that dense pretrained models can be transformed into sparse MoE variants, but prior studies have focused on large models and required extensive additional training. In this work, we demonstrate that MoE upcycling is also effective for small language models (sub-billion parameters) using only a few hundred thousand samples of supervised fine-tuning. Remarka
