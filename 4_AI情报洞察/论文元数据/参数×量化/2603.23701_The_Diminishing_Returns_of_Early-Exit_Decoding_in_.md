## 摘要

In Large Language Model (LLM) inference, early-exit refers to stopping computation at an intermediate layer once the prediction is sufficiently confident, thereby reducing latency and cost. However, recent LLMs adopt improved pretraining recipes and architectures that reduce layer redundancy, potentially limiting early-exit opportunities.
