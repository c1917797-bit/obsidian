As deep learning models scale, managing, inspecting, and modifying large checkpoints has become increasingly challenging. Researchers often need to alter model weights for layer restructuring, precision casting, low-rank factorization, and architectural debugging, yet these workflows often rely on fragile ad-hoc Python scripts. Here, we introduce BrainSurgery, a tool for robust and reproducible "tensor surgery" on neural network checkpoints, and provide a system demonstration covering four examples and three case studies from model upcycling to LoRA extraction.
