## 摘要

Large reasoning models (LRMs) such as DeepSeek-R1 have achieved strong performance through extended chain-of-thought (CoT) generation. However, deploying them on edge devices raises a conflict between long CoT sequences and constrained resources. Recent confidence-based early exit methods reduce CoT length for individual requests, yet they apply fixed thresholds from a single-request perspective, ignoring multi-request concurrency and load fluctuation in edge serving.
