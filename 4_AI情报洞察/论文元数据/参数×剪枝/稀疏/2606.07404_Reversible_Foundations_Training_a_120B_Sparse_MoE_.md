## 摘要

This paper reports on training a hundred-billion-parameter sparse mixture of experts on a single eight-GPU node, end to end. LightningLM 0.1V is a recurrence-backbone language model family grown in four stages from a small dense seed, through a 5B and a 9B mixture of experts, to a 120B model with 460 routed experts under top-12 routing. Each larger model is grown from the trained weights of the smaller one; active parameters rise monotonically from 1.78B at the dense seed to 5.93B at 120B (about 5% of the 118.67B stored).
