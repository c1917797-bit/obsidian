## 摘要

We present Activation Residual Hessian Quantization (ARHQ), a post-training weight splitting method designed to mitigate error propagation in low-bit activation-weight quantization. By constructing an input-side residual Hessian from activation quantization residuals (G_x), ARHQ analytically identifies and isolates error-sensitive weight directions into a high-precision low-rank branch. This is achieved via a closed-form truncated SVD on the scaled weight matrix W G^{1/2}_x .
