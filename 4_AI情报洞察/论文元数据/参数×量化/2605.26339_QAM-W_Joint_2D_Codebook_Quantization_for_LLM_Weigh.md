## 摘要

Scalar post-training quantizers discard pairwise coordinate structure within weight rows. We introduce QAM-W (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation for Weights), a codec that recovers this structure: each row is L2-normalized, block-Hadamard rotated, paired into 2D coordinates, and quantized against a single Lloyd-Max codebook trained on the unit circular Gaussian, with activation-aware per-channel scaling. In a cross-model study spanning five LLMs from four families (1.1B--13B parameters) and eight quantized configurations, the activation-aware variant at $\approx 5.5$ bpw stays within $\pm 0.4\%$
