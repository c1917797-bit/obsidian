## 摘要

Modern Transformer-based models frequently suffer from miscalibration, producing overconfident predictions that do not reflect true empirical frequencies. This work investigates the calibration dynamics of LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation and a novel hyper-network-based adaptation framework as parameter-efficient alternatives to full fine-tuning for RoBERTa. Evaluating across the GLUE benchmark, we demonstrate that LoRA-based adaptation consistently achieves calibration parity with (and in specific tasks exceeds) full fine-tuning, while maintaining significantly higher parameter efficiency.
