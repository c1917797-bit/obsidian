## 摘要

Data-free continual model merging (DFCMM) aims to fuse independently fine-tuned models into a single backbone that evolves with incoming tasks without accessing task data. This paper revisits two fundamental desiderata for DFCMM: stability, avoiding interference with earlier tasks, and plasticity, adapting faithfully to each new task. This poses a challenge that existing approaches fail to address: how to bridge data-level desiderata with parameter-space optimization to ensure stability and plasticity in the absence of task data.
