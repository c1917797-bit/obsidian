## 摘要

Can a transformer learn which attention entries matter during training? In principle, yes: attention distributions are highly concentrated, and a small gate network can identify the important entries post-hoc with near-perfect accuracy. In practice, barely. When sparse attention is trained end-to-end, the model's Q/K/V projections co-adapt to whatever mask is imposed, absorbing the routing signal until learned gates perform little better than frozen random gates.
