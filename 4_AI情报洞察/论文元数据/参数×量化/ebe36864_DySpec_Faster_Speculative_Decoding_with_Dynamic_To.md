## 摘要

While speculative decoding has recently appeared as a promising direction for accelerating the inference of large language models (LLMs), the speedup and scalability are strongly bounded by the token acceptance rate.
Prevalent methods usually organize predicted tokens as independent chains or fixed token trees, which fails to generalize to diverse query distributions. 
In this paper, we propose \textsc{DySpec}, a faster speculative decoding algorithm with a novel dynamic token tree structure.
