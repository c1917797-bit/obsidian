## 摘要

Federated learning enables collaborative model training across distributed clients while preserving their data privacy. However, privacy leakage and data heterogeneity remain significant challenges in federated learning. On the one hand, privacy leakage arises when the exposed information about client models during the client-server communication is exploited to reconstruct sensitive data or misuse client models, compromising both data and model privacy. On the other hand, data heterogeneity limits the generalization capability of the global model on clients, leading to suboptimal performance.
