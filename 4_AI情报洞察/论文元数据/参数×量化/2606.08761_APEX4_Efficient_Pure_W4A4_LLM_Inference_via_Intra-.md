## 摘要

W4A4 quantization promises full utilization of INT4 Tensor Cores, yet group dequantization overhead on CUDA Cores has driven existing systems to mixed-precision fallbacks. We present the first systematic study of how intra-SM compute balance governs this bottleneck. Through controlled benchmarks across four GPUs from Ampere and Ada architectures, we identify the Tensor Cores to CUDA Cores throughput ratio ($ρ$) as the primary hardware indicator: the W4A4-g128 kernel yields $2.0$--$2.5\times$ speedup on RTX~3090 ($ρ=16$) yet degrades to $0.43$--$0.47\times$ on A100 ($ρ=64$) in compute-bond scen
