## 摘要

We present FLOWR.root, an SE(3)-equivariant flow-matching model for pocket-aware 3D ligand generation with joint potency and binding affinity prediction and confidence estimation. The model supports de novo generation, interaction- and pharmacophore-conditional sampling, fragment elaboration and replacement, and multi-endpoint affinity prediction (pIC50, pKi, pKd, pEC50). Training combines large-scale ligand libraries with mixed-fidelity protein-ligand complexes, refined on curated co-crystal datasets and adapted to project-specific data through parameter-efficient finetuning.
