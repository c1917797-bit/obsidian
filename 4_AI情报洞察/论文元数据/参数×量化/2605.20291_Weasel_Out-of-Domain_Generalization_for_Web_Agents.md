Large language models (LLMs) have enabled web agents that follow natural language goals through multi-step browser interactions. However, agents fine-tuned on specific trajectories and domain often struggle to generalize out of domain, and offline training can be compute-inefficient due to noisy, redundant trajectories and long accessibility-tree (AXTree) states.
