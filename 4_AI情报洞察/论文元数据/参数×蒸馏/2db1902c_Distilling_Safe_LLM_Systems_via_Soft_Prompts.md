## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) have enabled machine learning to be integrated in complex tasks across various domains. This is a cause for concern since LLMs may respond to carefully crafted prompts with unsafe content, thus necessitating concrete safety mechanisms. Current solutions involve dual-model systems combining LLMs with guard models. However, the substantial memory and computational demands of guard models pose significant challenges for deployment.
