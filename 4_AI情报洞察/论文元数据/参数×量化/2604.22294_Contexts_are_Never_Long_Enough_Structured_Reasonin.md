## 摘要

Real-world document question answering is challenging. Analysts must synthesize evidence across multiple documents and different parts of each document. However, any fixed LLM context window can be exceeded as document collections grow. A common workaround is to decompose documents into chunks and assemble answers from chunk-level outputs, but this introduces an aggregation bottleneck: as the number of chunks grows, systems must still combine and reason over an increasingly large body of extracted evidence.
