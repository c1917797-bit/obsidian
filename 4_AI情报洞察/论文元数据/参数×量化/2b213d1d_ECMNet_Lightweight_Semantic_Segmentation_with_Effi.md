## 摘要

In the past decade, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Transformers have achieved wide application in semantic segmentation tasks. Although CNNs with Transformer models greatly improve performance, the global context modeling remains inadequate. Recently, Mamba achieved great potential in vision tasks, showing its advantages in modeling long-range dependency. In this paper, we propose a lightweight Efficient CNN-Mamba Network for semantic segmentation, dubbed as ECMNet.  ECMNet combines CNN with Mamba skillfully in a capsule-based framework to address their complementary weaknesses. Spec
