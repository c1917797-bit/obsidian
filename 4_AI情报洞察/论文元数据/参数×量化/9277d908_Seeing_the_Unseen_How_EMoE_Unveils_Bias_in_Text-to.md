## 摘要

Estimating uncertainty in text-to-image diffusion models is challenging due to their massive parameter counts (often exceeding 100M) and operation in complex, high-dimensional spaces with virtually unbounded input domains. We introduce Epistemic Mixture of Experts (EMoE), a framework for efficient estimation of epistemic uncertainty in diffusion models. EMoE leverages pre-trained networks without requiring additional training, enabling direct uncertainty estimation from a prompt.
