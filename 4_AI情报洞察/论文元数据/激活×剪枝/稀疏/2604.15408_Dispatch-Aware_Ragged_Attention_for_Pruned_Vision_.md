## 摘要

Token pruning methods for Vision Transformers (ViTs) promise quadratic reductions in attention FLOPs by dropping uninformative patches. Yet standard variable-length attention APIs -- including FlashAttention-2's varlen and PyTorch's NestedTensor SDPA -- fail to translate these savings into proportional wall-clock gains at the short post-pruning sequence lengths typical of ViTs ($\leq$197 tokens). We identify a dispatch-overhead bottleneck: at these lengths, host-side kernel dispatch consumes ${\sim}$50\,$μ$s regardless of workload, exceeding the actual GPU compute time at moderate-to-high prun
