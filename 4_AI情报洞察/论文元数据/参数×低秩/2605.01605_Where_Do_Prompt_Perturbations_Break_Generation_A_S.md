## 摘要

Large language models are sensitive to minor prompt perturbations, yet existing robustness methods usually enforce consistency at the whole-sequence level. This holistic view can hide an important failure mode: a perturbed response may remain globally similar to the clean one while drifting on a critical entity, relation, or conclusion. We introduce S$^2$R$^2$, a segment-level framework for robust LoRA fine-tuning.
