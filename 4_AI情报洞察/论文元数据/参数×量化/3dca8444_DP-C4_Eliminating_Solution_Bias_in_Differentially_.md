## 摘要

Differentially private (DP) stochastic optimization algorithms are widely used in privacy-preserving deep learning, where per-sample gradient clipping and noise injection protect sensitive information. However, these operations limit existing DP methods to converge within a constant-radius neighborhood of the first-order stationary point, leading to solution bias and the well-known privacy-utility trade-off. To enhance model utility, we propose a novel framework called DP-C4, which is designed to be error-Consistently-decayed, Coupledly-clipped, solution-Calibrated, and Convergence-guaranteed;
