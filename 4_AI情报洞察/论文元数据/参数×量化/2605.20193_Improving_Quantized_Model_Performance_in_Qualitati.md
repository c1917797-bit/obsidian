Quantized Large Language Models (LLMs) are used more often in qualitative analysis because they run fast and need fewer computing resources. This study examines how different lower bits quantization levels (8-bit, 4-bit, 3-bit, and 2-bit) and quantization types affect the performance of LLaMA-3.1 (8B) on qualitative analysis. The study uses expert and non-expert responses from 82 interview transcripts.
