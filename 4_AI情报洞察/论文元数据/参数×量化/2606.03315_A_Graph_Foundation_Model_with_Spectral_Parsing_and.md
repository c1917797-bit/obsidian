## 摘要

Graph foundation models aim to learn transferable knowledge from diverse graphs for generalization to unseen graphs and tasks. Unlike text and images, graphs lack a shared vocabulary or regular spatial grid, making cross-graph transfer challenging. This challenge comes from both feature discrepancies and, more critically, diverse graph structures.
