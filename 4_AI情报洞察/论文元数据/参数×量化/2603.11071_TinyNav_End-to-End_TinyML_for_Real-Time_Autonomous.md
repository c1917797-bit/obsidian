## 摘要

Autonomous navigation typically relies on power-intensive processors, limiting accessibility in low-cost robotics. Although microcontrollers offer a resource-efficient alternative, they impose strict constraints on model complexity. We present TinyNav, an end-to-end TinyML system for real-time autonomous navigation on an ESP32 microcontroller. A custom-trained, quantized 2D convolutional neural network processes a 20-frame sliding window of depth data to predict steering and throttle commands.
