## 摘要

We present \textbf{Flash-GMM}, a fused Triton kernel for efficient computation of Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs) over large-scale data in a single GPU pass. By eliminating the need to materialize the full responsibility matrix in GPU memory, Flash-GMM achieves a \textbf{20$\times$} speedup over existing implementations and enables training on datasets more than \textbf{100$\times$} larger than previously feasible on one device.
