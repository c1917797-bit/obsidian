## 摘要

Passive Acoustic Monitoring (PAM) is widely used for biodiversity assessment. Its application in African tropical forests is limited by scarce annotated data, reducing the performance of general-purpose ecoacoustic models on underrepresented taxa. In this study, we introduce DeepForestSound (DFS), a multi-species automatic detection model designed for PAM in African tropical forests.
