## 摘要

K-means clustering is a workhorse of unsupervised learning, but it is notoriously brittle to outliers, distribution shifts, and limited sample sizes. Viewing k-means as Lloyd--Max quantization of the empirical distribution, we develop a distributionally robust variant that protects against such pathologies. We posit that the unknown population distribution lies within a Wasserstein-2 ball around the empirical distribution. In this setting, one seeks cluster centers that minimize the worst-case expected squared distance over this ambiguity set, leading to a minimax formulation. A tractable dual
