## 摘要

We introduce RKSC (Reasoning-Aware KV Cache Sharing), a training-free inference framework that eliminates two structural redundancies in multi-branch LLM reasoning pipelines. ASKS (Attention-Similarity KV Sharing) computes the prefix KV cache once and broadcasts it to all semantically similar branches via hidden-state cosine similarity, strictly generalising the token-exact prefix caching used by vLLM and SGLang. CGEE (Confidence-Gated Early Exit) applies two complementary exit mechanisms: (1) it skips the verification forward pass entirely when generation confidence is decisive across branche
