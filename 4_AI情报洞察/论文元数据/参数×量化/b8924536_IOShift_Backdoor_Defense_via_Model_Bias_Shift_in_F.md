As a privacy-preserving and decentralized machine learning framework, Federated Learning (FL) is vulnerable to backdoor attacks. Current backdoor defenses rely on a strong assumption: defenders have the ability of defining a benign parameter space using gradient information to detect or remove malicious updates. However, in the real-world not-independent-and-identically-distributed (Non-IID) FL scenarios, this is a particularly challenging task, exhibiting inconsistent performance across different systems and settings.
