## 摘要

Local deployment of large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models falls short of the service quality achieved in cloud-scale environments, even under low-concurrency workloads. We identify four key gaps in local MoE inference: reliance on capacity-reduced models (quantized, distilled, rerouted), inability to meet 30-second TTFT for long prefills (more than 12K), sub-baseline decode throughput (under 20 tokens/s), and poor concurrency under mixed prefill-decode and batched decode workloads. We present a CPU-GPU hybrid system that achieves cloud-level SLOs on dual-socket commodity CPUs and consumer GPUs
