## 摘要

Multi-model LLM routing has emerged as an effective approach for reducing serving cost and latency while maintaining output quality by assigning each prompt to an appropriate model. However, prior routing methods typically assume that each model has a fixed latency. In real deployments, this assumption is inaccurate: multiple models often share limited GPU resources, and a model's latency depends strongly on both its allocated resources and the request load induced by the routing policy.
