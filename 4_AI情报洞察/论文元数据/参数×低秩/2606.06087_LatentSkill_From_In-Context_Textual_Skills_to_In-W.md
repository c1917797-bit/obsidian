## 摘要

Agent systems increasingly use textual skills to encode reusable task procedures, but injecting these skills into the prompt at every step incurs substantial context overhead and exposes skill content as plaintext. We present LatentSkill, a framework that converts textual skills into plug-and-play LoRA adapters through a pretrained hypernetwork. LatentSkill stores skill knowledge in weight space rather than context space, removing per-step skill tokens while preserving modular loading, scaling, and composition. On ALFWorld and Search-QA, LatentSkill outperforms the corresponding in-context ski
