## 摘要

Post-training quantization (PTQ) compresses large language models by mapping weights to low-bit representations. The scaling factor that defines the quantization grid is typically chosen using simple, data-free heuristics. In this work, we present PiSO (Piecewise Scale Optimization), an algorithm that leverages calibration data to compute the optimal channel-wise weight scales exactly and efficiently under round-to-nearest quantization.
