## 摘要

Separating multiple effects in time series is fundamental yet challenging for time-series forecasting (TSF). However, existing TSF models cannot effectively learn interpretable multi-effect decomposition by their smoothing-based temporal techniques. Here, a new interpretable frequency-based decomposition pipeline MLOW captures the insight: a time series can be represented as a magnitude spectrum multiplied by the corresponding phase-aware basis functions, and the magnitude spectrum distribution of a time series always exhibits observable patterns for different effects.
