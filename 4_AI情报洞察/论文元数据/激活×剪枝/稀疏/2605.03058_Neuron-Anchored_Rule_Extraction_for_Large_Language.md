## 摘要

A central goal of explainable AI is to express large language model (LLM) decision logic symbolically and ground it in internal mechanisms. Existing rule-extraction methods usually learn ungrounded symbolic surrogates, while mechanistic interpretability links behavior to neurons but often requires hand-crafted hypotheses and costly interventions. We introduce MechaRule, a pipeline that grounds rule extraction in LLM circuits by localizing sparse agonist activations whose ablation disrupts rule-related behavior.
