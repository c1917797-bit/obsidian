## 摘要

Scientific time series are central to scientific AI but are typically sparse, highly heterogeneous, and limited in scale, making unified representation learning particularly challenging. Meanwhile, foundation models pretrained on relevant time series domains such as audio, general time series, and brain signals contain rich knowledge, but their applicability to scientific signals remains underexplored. In this paper, we investigate the transferability and complementarity of foundation models from relevant time series domains, and study how to effectively leverage them to build a unified encode
