## 摘要

We introduce VISE, a novel family of numerical integration method that combines symbolic regression with structure-preserving variational 
integrators to efficiently solve Lagrangian and Hamiltonian ordinary differential equations. Unlike general symbolic regression methods, which seek a single static closed-form expression that may diverge outside the training data or fail to form a valid expression, VISE operates as a hybrid numerical integrator that combines the interpretability of symbolic expressions with the structure-preservation of variational methods.
