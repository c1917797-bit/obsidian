## 摘要

Code-switching (CS) speech translation (ST) aims to translate speech that alternates between multiple languages into a target language text, posing significant challenges due to the complexity of semantic modeling and the scarcity of CS data. Previous studies mainly rely on the models themselves to implicitly learn semantic representations and resort to costly manual annotations. To mitigate these limitations, we propose enhancing Large Language Models (LLMs) with a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) speech projector composed of language expert groups, where each group specializes in the semantic space
