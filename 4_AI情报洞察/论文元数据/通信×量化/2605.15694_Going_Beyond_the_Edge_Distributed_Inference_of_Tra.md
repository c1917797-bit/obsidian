## 摘要

Transformer models are rapidly becoming a cornerstone of modern Internet of Things (IoT) applications, yet their computational and memory demands far exceed the capabilities of a single typical ultra-low-power IoT device. We present CATS, a framework for distributed transformer inference on ultra-low-power wireless devices, enabling multiple devices to collaboratively execute models far larger than what a single device can sustain. At its core, CATS is a communication-aware distributed transformer inference scheme co-designed across transformer partitioning, wireless communication and training
