## 摘要

Dataset Distillation (DD) compresses large datasets into compact synthetic ones that maintain training performance. However, current methods mainly target sample reduction, with limited consideration of data precision and its impact on efficiency. We propose Quantization-aware Dataset Distillation (QuADD), a unified framework that jointly optimizes dataset compactness and precision under fixed bit budgets.
