## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) are trained on vast datasets that may contain sensitive information. Differential privacy (DP), the de facto standard for formal privacy guarantees, provides a principled framework for training LLMs with provable privacy protection. However, state-of-the-art DP training implementations rely on fast gradient clipping techniques with memory overhead $O(B \min\{T^2, d^2\})$, where $B$ is the batch size, $T$ is the sequence length, and $d$ is the model width.
