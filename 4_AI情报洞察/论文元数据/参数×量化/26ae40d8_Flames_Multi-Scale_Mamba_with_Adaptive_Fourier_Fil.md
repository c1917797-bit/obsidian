## 摘要

Time series data usually exhibit intricate characteristics such as non-stationarity, noise, multi-scale periodicity, and transient dynamics, posing significant challenges to long-term time series forecasting (LTSF). While transformer-based models effectively capture long-range dependencies, their practical applications are hindered by high computational cost with quadratic complexity, noise sensitivity, and overfitting on small datasets. Moreover, time series present distinct patterns at different temporal resolutions, containing both fine-grained (micro) and coarse-grained (macro) information
