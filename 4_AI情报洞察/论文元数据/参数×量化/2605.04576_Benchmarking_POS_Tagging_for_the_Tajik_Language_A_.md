## 摘要

This paper presents the first benchmark for the task of automatic part-of-speech (POS) tagging for the Tajik language. Despite the existence of multilingual language models demonstrating high effectiveness for many of the world's languages, their capacity for grammatical analysis of Tajik has remained unexplored until now. The aim of this study is to fill this gap through a systematic comparison of classical neural network architectures and modern multilingual transformers.   Experiments were conducted on the TajPersParallel corpus, a parallel lexical resource comprising approximately 44,000 d
