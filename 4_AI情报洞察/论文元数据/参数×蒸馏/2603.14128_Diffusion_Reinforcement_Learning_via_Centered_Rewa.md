## 摘要

Diffusion and flow models achieve State-Of-The-Art (SOTA) generative performance, yet many practically important behaviors such as fine-grained prompt fidelity, compositional correctness, and text rendering are weakly specified by score or flow matching pretraining objectives. Reinforcement Learning (RL) fine-tuning with external, black-box rewards is a natural remedy, but diffusion RL is often brittle. Trajectory-based methods incur high memory cost and high-variance gradient estimates; forward-process approaches converge faster but can suffer from distribution drift, and hence reward hacking
