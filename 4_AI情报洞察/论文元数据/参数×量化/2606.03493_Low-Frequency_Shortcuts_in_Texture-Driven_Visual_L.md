## 摘要

Neural networks suffer from shortcut learning, where learned features generalize well to the training set but not to in-distribution (ID) or out-of-distribution (OOD) test sets. Existing studies are all based on a few standard benchmarks, which are shape-driven. Numerous application domains, however, are texture-driven. In this work, we present shortcut learning analysis for texture-driven domains, and compare it with that of a standard benchmark.
