## 摘要

On-device adaptation of large language models commonly keeps a quantized base model frozen while training and deploying a small, task-specific LoRA adapter. In the unmerged adapter-mode setting, however, the adapter is more than a compact storage module; it introduces an additional dense floating-point branch, maintains a trainable state for local updates, and acts as a unit of communication and hot-swapping.We introduce LoRDBA, a LoRA-compatible adapter that replaces both low-rank factors with binary sign carriers while representing magnitudes through lightweight, channel-wise scales, convert
