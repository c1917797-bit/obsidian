## 摘要

Serving large language model (LLM) inference in cloud environments requires jointly optimizing model selection, GPU provisioning, parallelism configuration, and workload routing under latency, accuracy, memory, and budget constraints. While mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) can model this problem, its computational cost limits frequent re-optimization under demand variability. Existing heuristics often optimize individual components separately and may become infeasible when system-wide constraints are enforced.   This paper presents a scalable framework for SLO-constrained LLM inference.
