Channel estimation is crucial in 5G communication networks for optimizing transmission parameters and ensuring reliable, high-speed communication. However, the use of multiple-input and multiple-output (MIMO) and millimeter-wave (mmWave) in 5G networks presents challenges in achieving accurate estimation under strict latency requirements on resource-limited hardware platforms. To address these challenges, we propose SwiftChannel, an algorithm-hardware co-design framework that integrates a hardware-friendly deep learning-based channel estimator with a dedicated accelerator.
