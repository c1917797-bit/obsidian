## 摘要

How cost-effectively can strong reasoning abilities be achieved in language models? Driven by this question, we present Tina, a family of tiny reasoning models achieved with high cost-efficiency. Tina shows that substantial reasoning performance can be developed using only minimal resources, by applying low-rank adaptation (LoRA) during reinforcement learning (RL), to an already tiny 1.5B parameter base model.
