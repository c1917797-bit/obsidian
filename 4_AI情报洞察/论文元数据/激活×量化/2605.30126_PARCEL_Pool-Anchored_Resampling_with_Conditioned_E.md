## 摘要

Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) map visual inputs into dense token sequences, imposing a quadratic computational bottleneck for inference. Elastic visual-token compression addresses this by training a single model that can run at multiple visual-token budgets. However, existing approaches struggle under aggressive compression. Spatial-only compression, as in nested pooling, behaves as an imperfect low-pass filter and induces spectral aliasing that obscures fine-grained detail.
