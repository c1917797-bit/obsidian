## 摘要

Low-rank adaptation (LoRA) has become the dominant paradigm for parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) of large language models (LLMs). However, its bilinear structure introduces a critical limitation: the mapping from trainable parameters to weight updates is not distance-preserving, distorting the optimization landscape.
