## 摘要

We present PolarQuant, a post-training weight quantization method for large language models (LLMs) that exploits the distributional structure of neural network weights to achieve near-lossless compression. PolarQuant operates in three stages: (1) block-wise normalization to the unit hypersphere, (2) Walsh-Hadamard rotation to transform coordinates into approximately Gaussian random variables, and (3) quantization with centroids matched to the Gaussian distribution.
