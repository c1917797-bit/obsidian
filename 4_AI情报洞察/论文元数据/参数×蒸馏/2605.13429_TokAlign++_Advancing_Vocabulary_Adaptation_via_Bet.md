## 摘要

Tokenization is a foundational step in the text process of Large Language Models (LLMs). Texts must be first tokenized into token IDs, which are then input to LLMs. Inefficient tokenization results in long token-ID sequences and will slow down the training and inference of LLMs. The fine-grained knowledge transfer between LLMs, like token-level distillation, is also impeded by the mismatch in vocabulary.
