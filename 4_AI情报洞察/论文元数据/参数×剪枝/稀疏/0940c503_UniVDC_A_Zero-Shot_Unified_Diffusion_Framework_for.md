## 摘要

Recovering metrically consistent and temporally stable depth from dynamic videos remains challenging, particularly when sparse, noisy measurements coexist with structural voids, occlusion reveals, motion drift, and sensor dropouts. Under these conditions, single-frame methods lack temporal correction while existing video depth estimation approaches underutilize explicit sparse geometry, leading to scale drift and flicker.
