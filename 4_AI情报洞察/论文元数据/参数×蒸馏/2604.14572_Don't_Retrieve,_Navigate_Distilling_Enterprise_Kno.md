## 摘要

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds LLM responses in external evidence but treats the model as a passive consumer of search results, with no view of how the corpus is organized or what it has not yet seen. We present Corpus2Skill, which distills a document corpus offline into a hierarchical skill directory and lets an LLM agent navigate it at serve time, drilling from a bird's-eye view through progressively finer summaries down to documents, and backtracking when a branch is unproductive.
