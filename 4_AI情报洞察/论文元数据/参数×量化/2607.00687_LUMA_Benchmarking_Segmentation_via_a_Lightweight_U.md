## 摘要

Comparing transformer backbones for image segmentation is confounded: each is paired with a different decoder, recipe, and pretraining, so reported differences rarely reflect the backbone itself. We introduce the Lightweight Universal Mask Adapter (LUMA), a lightweight, backbone-agnostic mask-transformer head that treats any backbone as a black-box feature extractor, letting a set of queries read from its features through cheap cross-attention.
