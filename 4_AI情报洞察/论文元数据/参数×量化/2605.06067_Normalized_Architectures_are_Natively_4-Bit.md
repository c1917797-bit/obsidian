## 摘要

Training large language models at 4-bit precision is critical for efficiency. We show that nGPT, an architecture that constrains weights and hidden representations to the unit hypersphere, is inherently more robust to low-precision arithmetic. This removes the need for interventions-such as applying random Hadamard transforms and performing per-tensor scaling calculations-to preserve model quality, and it enables stable end-to-end NVFP4 training.
