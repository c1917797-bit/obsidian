Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have demonstrated outstanding performance across a variety of domains. However, training MLLMs is often inefficient, as much of the computation is redundant due to the long input sequences from multimodal data and underutilized inter-layer operations. Notably, such redundancy is not static but varies across different stages of training.
