## 摘要

Serving large language models under latency service-level objectives (SLOs) is a configuration-heavy systems problem with an unusually failure-prone search space: many plausible configurations crash outright or miss user-visible latency targets, and standard black-box optimizers treat these failures as wasted trials. We present SLO-Guard, a crash-aware autotuner for vLLM serving that treats crashes as first-class observations.
