## 摘要

NeuroFlex is a column-level accelerator that co-executes artificial and spiking neural networks to minimize energy-delay product on sparse edge workloads with competitive accuracy. The design extends integer-exact QCFS ANN-SNN conversion from layers to independent columns. It unifies INT8 storage with on-the-fly spike generation using an offline cost model to assign columns to ANN or SNN cores and pack work across processing elements with deterministic runtime. Our cost-guided scheduling algorithm improves throughput by 16-19% over random mapping and lowers EDP by 57-67% versus a strong ANN-on
