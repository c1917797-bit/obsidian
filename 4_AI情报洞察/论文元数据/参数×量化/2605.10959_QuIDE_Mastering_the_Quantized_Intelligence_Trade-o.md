## 摘要

There is currently no unified metric for evaluating the efficiency of quantized neural networks. We propose QuIDE, built around the Intelligence Index I = (C x P)/log_2(T+1), which collapses the compression-accuracy-latency trade-off into a single score. Experiments across six settings -- SimpleCNN (MNIST, CIFAR), ResNet-18 (ImageNet-1K), and Llama-3-8B -- show a task-dependent Pareto Knee. 4-bit quantization is optimal for MNIST and large LLMs, while 8-bit is the sweet spot for complex CNN tasks (ResNet-18 on ImageNet), where 4-bit PTQ collapses accuracy catastrophically.
