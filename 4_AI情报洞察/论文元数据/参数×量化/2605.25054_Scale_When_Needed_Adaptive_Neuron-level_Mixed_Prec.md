## 摘要

Deploying deep neural networks on resource-constrained 6G edge devices demands aggressive compression with minimal accuracy loss. Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) has emerged as a leading compression approach; however, existing mixed-precision methods typically operate at coarse layer- or channel-level granularity. These methods often rely on heuristic or search-based bit-allocation strategies, which may overlook fine-grained variability at the neuron level. We propose Neuron-Level Mixed-Precision QAT (NMP-QAT), where each neuron independently learns its own discrete precision during training
