## 摘要

Accurate grid load forecasting is safety-critical: under-predictions risk supply shortfalls, while symmetric error metrics can mask this operational asymmetry. We introduce an operator-legible evaluation framework -- Under-Prediction Rate (UPR), tail $\text{Reserve}_{99.5}^{\%}$ requirements, and explicit inflation diagnostics ($\text{Bias}_{24h}$/OPR) -- to quantify one-sided reliability risk beyond MAPE.   Using this framework, we evaluate five neural architectures -- two state space models (S-Mamba, PowerMamba), two Transformers (iTransformer, PatchTST), an LSTM, and a probabilistic SSM var
