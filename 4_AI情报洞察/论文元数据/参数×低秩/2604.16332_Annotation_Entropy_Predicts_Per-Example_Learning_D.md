## 摘要

We find that LoRA fine-tuning exhibits un-learning on contested examples: items with high annotator disagreement show increasing loss during training, a qualitatively distinct pattern largely absent under full fine-tuning and consistent across all six models tested (four encoder, two decoder-only). This discovery emerges from correlating annotation entropy, computed from ChaosNLI's 100 labels per example, with per-example area under the loss curve (AULC) on SNLI and MNLI.
