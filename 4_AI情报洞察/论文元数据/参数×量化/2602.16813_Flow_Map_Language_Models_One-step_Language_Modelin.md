## 摘要

Language models based on discrete diffusion have attracted widespread interest for their potential to provide faster generation than autoregressive models. Despite their promise, these models typically produce samples whose quality sharply degrades in the few-step regime, preventing a dramatic speedup in practice. Here, we show that language models based on continuous flows over one-hot token embeddings can outperform discrete diffusion in both quality and speed.
