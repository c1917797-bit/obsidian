## 摘要

Semi-structured 2:4 sparsity is widely supported by modern accelerators, providing up to a 2x theoretical speedup. However, its strict 50% sparsity constraint often causes non-negligible accuracy degradation under post-training pruning. Meanwhile, existing relaxed sparsity formats either require specialized compiler support or introduce runtime overheads that limit end-to-end speedup. We propose Spense, a practical hybrid sparse-dense format that splits each weight matrix into a 2:4 sparse region and a dense region.
