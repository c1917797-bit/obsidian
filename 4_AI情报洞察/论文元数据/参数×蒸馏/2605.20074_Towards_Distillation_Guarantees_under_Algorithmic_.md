## 摘要

Distillation transfers knowledge from a large model trained on broad data to a smaller, more efficient model suitable for deployment. In structured prediction settings, prior knowledge about the task can guide the choice of a target architecture that is algorithmically aligned with the underlying problem. Building on recent learning-theoretic analyses of decision-tree (DT) distillation (Boix-Adsera, 2024), we study when distillation succeeds for combinatorial optimization tasks.
