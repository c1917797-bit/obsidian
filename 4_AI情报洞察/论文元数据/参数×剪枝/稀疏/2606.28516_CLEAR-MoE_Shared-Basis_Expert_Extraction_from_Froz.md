## 摘要

We present CLEAR-MoE, a four-phase post-training pipeline that converts a frozen pretrained Vision Transformer (ViT) into a sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model without updating backbone weights. The pipeline (i) scores feed-forward network (FFN) layers by sparsity, clusterability, and output sensitivity; (ii) decomposes selected layers into a shared low-rank SVD basis and per-cluster residual experts using k-means clustering; (iii) trains lightweight routers supervised by cluster labels; and (iv) dispatches tokens through pluggable CUDA backends.
