## 摘要

System Neural Diversity (SND) measures behavioral heterogeneity in multi-agent reinforcement learning by averaging pairwise distances over all $\binom{n}{2}$ agent pairs, making each call quadratic in team size. We introduce Graph-SND, which replaces this complete-graph average with a weighted average over the edges of an arbitrary graph $G$.
