## 摘要

Low-rank decomposition is a compelling approach for compressing large language models, but its effectiveness hinges on selecting which singular-vector bases to retain for a target task. Existing methods such as Basel adapt singular-value coefficients on downstream data and prune bases with small re-learned magnitudes, a heuristic that can be misaligned with task performance because it ignores the local geometry of the loss landscape. We present Basis Selection with Importance (BSI), a principled low-rank compression framework that ranks and prunes bases by directly estimating the expected loss
