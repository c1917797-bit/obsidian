## 摘要

We study how to allocate a fixed supervised fine-tuning budget when three objectives must be balanced at once: multi-turn safety alignment, low over-refusal on benign boundary queries, and instruction following under verifiable constraints. We propose MOSAIC (Multi-Objective Slice-Aware Iterative Curation for Alignment), a multi-objective framework for closed-loop data mixture search built on a unified L1-L3 evaluation interface. MOSAIC turns slice-level failure profiles into executable data actions, including dataset-level mixture ratios, bucket-level weights, and focus criteria. Under a fixe
