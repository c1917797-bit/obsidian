Among the methods employed for inference in these models, sampling-based Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) techniques are widely used. However, MCMC methods come with significant computational costs and are inherently challenging to parallelize, resulting in inefficient execution on conventional CPU/GPU platforms.
