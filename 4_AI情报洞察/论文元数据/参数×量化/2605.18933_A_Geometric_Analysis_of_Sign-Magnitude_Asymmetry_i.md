## 摘要

Pre-norm Transformers with RMSNorm tolerate ternary {-1,0,+1} weight quantization with surprisingly small loss (Ma et al., 2024). We give a geometric explanation via sign-magnitude decomposition of weight perturbations. In a two-layer ReLU + RMSNorm model with i.i.d. Gaussian weights, sign-flips produce $π/(π-2) \approx 2.75$ times more transverse output energy than sign-preserving magnitude perturbations of equal Frobenius norm, as the flip rate $p \to 0$ (Theorem 3).
