## 摘要

It is shown that a popular GPTQ algorithm may have an arbitrarily large gap to the IT limit. To alleviate this problem, a novel algorithm, termed ``WaterSIC'', is proposed and is shown to be within a rate gap of 0.255 bits to the IT limit, uniformly over all possible covariance matrices of input activations.
