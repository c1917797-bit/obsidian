## 摘要

While large language models provide strong compositional reasoning, existing reasoning segmentation pipelines fail to transparently connect this reasoning to visual perception. Current methods, such as latent query alignment, are end-to-end yet opaque "black boxes". Conversely, textual localization readout is merely readable, not truly interpretable, often functioning as an unconstrained post-hoc step.
