## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated exceptional capabilities across a wide range of tasks, yet they remain susceptible to jailbreaking attacks, where adversarial prompts are crafted to bypass the models' safety mechanisms and generate harmful outputs. Traditional jailbreaking methods often rely on static templates or inefficient processes, resulting in limited diversity and effectiveness.
