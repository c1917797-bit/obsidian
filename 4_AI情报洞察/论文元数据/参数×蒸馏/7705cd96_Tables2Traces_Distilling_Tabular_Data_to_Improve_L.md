## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) excel at reasoning when fine-tuned on curated text corpora, but many domains, such as medicine, primarily store knowledge in structured tabular data. Despite its richness, tabular data has been largely overlooked as a source of reasoning supervision. Interpreting such data requires structured, relational reasoning across features and outcomes, not just surface-level pattern matching.
