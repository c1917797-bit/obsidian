## 摘要

Although Large Language Models (LLMs) perform exceptionally well in general domains, the problem of hallucinations poses significant risks in specialized fields such as healthcare and law, where high interpretability is essential. Existing fine-tuning methods depend heavily on large-scale professional datasets, which are often hard to obtain due to the privacy regulations. Moreover, existing self-evolution methods are primarily designed for general domains, which may struggle to adapt to knowledge-intensive domains due to the lack of knowledge constraints.
