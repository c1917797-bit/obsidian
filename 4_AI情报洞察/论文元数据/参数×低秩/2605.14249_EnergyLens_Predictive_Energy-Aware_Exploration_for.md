## 摘要

We present EnergyLens, an end-to-end framework for energy-aware large language model (LLM) inference optimization. As LLMs scale, predicting and reducing their energy footprint has become critical for sustainability and datacenter operations, yet existing approaches either require production-level code and expensive profiling or fail to accurately capture multi-GPU energy behavior. As a result, practitioners lack tools for deciding which optimizations to prioritize and for selecting among existing deployment configurations when exhaustive profiling is impractical. EnergyLens addresses this gap
