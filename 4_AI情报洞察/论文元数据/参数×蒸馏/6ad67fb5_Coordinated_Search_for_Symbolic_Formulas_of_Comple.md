## 摘要

Distilling the dynamics of complex networks into symbolic formulas is a fundamental goal in science. However, existing neural symbolic regression methods often search for node (self-evolution) and edge (interaction) dynamics independently. This can lead to overfitting, where errors in one component are compensated for by an overly complex expression for the other, yielding uninterpretable and non-generalizable models.
