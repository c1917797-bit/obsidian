## 摘要

Deep neural networks are increasingly deployed across heterogeneous and partially untrusted environments, where models are distributed through cloud storage, CI/CD pipelines, containerized services, and edge execution platforms. This broad deployment landscape exposes model parameters to various integrity risks. Unlike input-space adversarial attacks, parameter attacks directly tamper with the model's internal parameters and persist across all subsequent inferences.
