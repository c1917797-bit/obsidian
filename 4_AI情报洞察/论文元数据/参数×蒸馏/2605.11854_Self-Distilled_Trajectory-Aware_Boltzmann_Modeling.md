## 摘要

Diffusion Language Models (DLMs) have recently emerged as a promising alternative to autoregressive language models, offering stronger global awareness and highly parallel generation. However, post-training DLMs with standard Negative Evidence Lower Bound (NELBO)-based supervised fine-tuning remains inefficient: training reconstructs randomly masked tokens in a single step, whereas inference follows a confidence-guided, multi-step easy-to-hard denoising trajectory. Recent trajectory-based self-distillation methods exploit such inference trajectories mainly for sampling-step compression and acc
