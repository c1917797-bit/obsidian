## 摘要

Training large-scale Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models is bottlenecked by activation memory and expert-parallel communication, yet FP4 training remains impractical on Hopper-class GPUs without native MXFP4 or NVFP4 support. In this work, we present a training recipe that enables MXFP4 efficiency for MoE models on Hopper architectures without native 4-bit computation support. A central challenge is to integrate FP4 into an existing BF16/FP8 hybrid training pipeline without incurring costly precision round-trips (e.g., FP4 $\leftrightarrow$ BF16 $\leftrightarrow$ FP8).
