## 摘要

The practical adoption of sampling-based inference (SAI) in Bayesian neural networks (BNNs) remains limited, partly due to persistent misconceptions about the feasibility and efficiency of sampling. This position paper argues that SAI has achieved computational parity with optimization-based methods and is at the verge of superseding such methods for effective and efficient inference in BNNs. This development should be in the interest of the whole community, promoting BNNs as a principled paradigm with its long-standing yet unfulfilled promise of providing principled uncertainty quantification
