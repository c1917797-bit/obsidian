## 摘要

Small language models (SLMs) often struggle with complex mathematical reasoning due to limited capacity to maintain long chains of intermediate steps and to recover from early errors. We address this challenge by introducing a hint-assisted reasoning framework that incrementally guides SLMs through multi-step mathematical problem solving. Our approach decomposes solutions into sequential reasoning steps and provides context-aware hints, where hints are generated by a separate SLM trained via distillation from a strong large language model.
