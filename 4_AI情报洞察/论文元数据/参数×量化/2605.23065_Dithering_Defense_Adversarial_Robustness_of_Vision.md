## 摘要

Vision foundation models are widely used as frozen backbones across many downstream tasks, making them a single point of failure under adversarial attack. We study multi-level Floyd-Steinberg error-diffusion dithering as a lightweight, model-agnostic input transformation that disrupts adversarial perturbations while preserving semantic content.
