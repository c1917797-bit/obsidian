## 摘要

Differentially private federated fine-tuning of large models with LoRA suffers from aggregation error caused by LoRA's multiplicative structure, which is further amplified by DP noise and degrades both stability and accuracy. Existing remedies apply a single update mode uniformly across all layers and all communication rounds (or alternate them on a fixed schedule), ignoring both the structural asymmetry between the two LoRA factors and the round-wise dynamics of training.
