## 摘要

We study what actually works and what doesn't for training large language models as agents via multi-turn reinforcement learning. We address this gap by first breaking down the design space into three inter-related pillars -- environment, reward, and policy -- and empirically derive a recipe for training LLM agents in situated textual domains.
