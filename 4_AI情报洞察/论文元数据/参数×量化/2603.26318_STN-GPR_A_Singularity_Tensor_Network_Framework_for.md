## 摘要

We develop a tensor-network surrogate for option pricing, targeting large-scale portfolio revaluation problems arising in market risk management (e.g., VaR and Expected Shortfall computations). The method involves representing high-dimensional price surfaces in tensor-train (TT) form using TT-cross approximation, constructing the surrogate directly from black-box price evaluations without materializing the full training tensor. For inference, we use a Laplacian kernel and derive TT representations of the kernel matrix and its closed-form inverse in the noise-free setting, enabling TT-based Gau
