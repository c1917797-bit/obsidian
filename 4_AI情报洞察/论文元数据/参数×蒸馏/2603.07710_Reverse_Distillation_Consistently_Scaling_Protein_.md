## 摘要

Unlike the predictable scaling laws in natural language processing and computer vision, protein language models (PLMs) scale poorly: for many tasks, models within the same family plateau or even decrease in performance, with mid-sized models often outperforming the largest in the family. We introduce Reverse Distillation, a principled framework that decomposes large PLM representations into orthogonal subspaces guided by smaller models of the same family.
