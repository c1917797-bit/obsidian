## 摘要

Model merging enables the reuse of fine-tuned models without joint training or access to original data. Dynamic merging further improves flexibility by selectively activating task-relevant parameters and efficiently composing experts across multiple tasks. However, existing dynamic methods either maintain a full shared model with tiny experts or allocate excessive capacity to experts, leading to suboptimal accuracy--efficiency trade-offs.
