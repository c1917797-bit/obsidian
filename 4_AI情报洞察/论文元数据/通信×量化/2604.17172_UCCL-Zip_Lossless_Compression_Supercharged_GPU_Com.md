## 摘要

The rapid growth of large language models (LLMs) has made GPU communication a critical bottleneck. While prior work reduces communication volume via quantization or lossy compression, these approaches introduce numerical errors that can degrade convergence, accuracy, and stability. We present UCCL-Zip, a unified design that integrates lossless compression directly into GPU communication primitives.
