## 摘要

Diffusion models are the go-to method for Text-to-Image generation, but their iterative denoising processes has high inference latency. Quantization reduces compute time by using lower bitwidths, but applies a fixed precision across all denoising timesteps, leaving an entire optimization axis unexplored.
