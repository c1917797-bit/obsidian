## 摘要

Low-rank gradient compression reduces communication in distributed training by representing updates with rank-$r$ factors. Dion is a recent method that approximates Muon, a spectral optimizer that orthogonalizes momentum, using one step of power iteration followed by column normalization (rescaling each column of the right factor to unit length). This makes it compatible with fully sharded data parallel training, but it converges more slowly than full-rank spectral methods.
