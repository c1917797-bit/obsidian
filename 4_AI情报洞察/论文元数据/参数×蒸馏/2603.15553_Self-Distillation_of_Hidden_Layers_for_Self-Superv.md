## 摘要

The landscape of self-supervised learning (SSL) is currently dominated by generative approaches (e.g., MAE) that reconstruct raw low-level data, and predictive approaches (e.g., I-JEPA) that predict high-level abstract embeddings. While generative methods provide strong grounding, they are computationally inefficient for high-redundancy modalities like imagery, and their training objective does not prioritize learning high-level, conceptual features.
