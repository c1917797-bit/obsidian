## 摘要

Pashto is absent from Whisper's pre-training corpus despite being one of CommonVoice's largest language collections, leaving off-the-shelf models unusable: all Whisper sizes output Arabic, Dari, or Urdu script on Pashto audio, achieving word error rates above 100%. We compare four fine-tuning strategies for whisper-base on CommonVoice Pashto v20: vanilla full fine-tuning, LoRA (rank 64), frozen-encoder (2/6 layers), and multistage Urdu-to-Pashto transfer.
