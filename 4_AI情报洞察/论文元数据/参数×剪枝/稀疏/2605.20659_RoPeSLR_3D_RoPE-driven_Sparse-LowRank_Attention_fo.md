While recent sparse-linear attention hybrids aim to mitigate this, their performance severely degrades at extreme sparsity due to the "RoPE Dilemma": standard linear attention fails to preserve the orthogonal relative-position structure of 3D Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), neutralizing vital distance awareness.
