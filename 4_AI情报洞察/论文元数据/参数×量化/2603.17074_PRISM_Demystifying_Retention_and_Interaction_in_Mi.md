## 摘要

We present PRISM, a comprehensive empirical study of mid-training design choices for large language models. Through controlled experiments across seven base models spanning four families (Granite, LLaMA, Mistral, Nemotron-H), two architecture types (dense Transformer and attention-Mamba hybrid), and scales from 3B to 24B parameters, we show that mid-training on approximately 27B high-quality tokens yields consistent gains of +15 to +40 points on math, +5 to +12 points on code, and +6 to +13 points on science benchmarks while preserving general performance.
