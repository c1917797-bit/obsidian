## 摘要

Encoder-decoder models offer substantial inference-time savings over decoder-only models, but their pretraining objectives suffer from sparse supervision and dynamic sequence lengths, keeping them out of practice at scale. We propose block-based double decoders, a novel transformer architecture that utilizes doubly-causal block-based attention masks to train with full loss supervision and static sequence packing, combining decoder-only training efficiency with encoder-decoder inference efficiency.
