## 摘要

Serial LLM inference backends -- such as Ollama -- process requests one at a time under FCFS admission, causing Head-of-Line Blocking (HOLB) under mixed workloads at high utilisation: short factual queries can be delayed by minutes behind long generation jobs. While cloud-scale deployments mitigate HOLB via continuous batching (vLLM, Orca), these solutions require tens of GB of VRAM for concurrent KV-caches -- infeasible for memory-constrained edge and local deployments that rely on serial request dispatch. We present \clairvoyant, a drop-in sidecar proxy for any serial OpenAI-compatible backe
