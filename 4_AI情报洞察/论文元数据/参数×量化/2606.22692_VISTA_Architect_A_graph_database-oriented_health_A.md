## 摘要

We introduce VISTA Architect, a database-oriented AI architecture for integrating large language models (LLMs) with longitudinal electronic health records (EHRs). At ingestion, it transforms complex clinical documentation into a persistent, provenance-linked knowledge graph, eliminating repeated reprocessing of raw records at query time. The architecture has two layers: a source-faithful MEDS Graph preserving granular EHR structure with full provenance, and a clinically abstracted Timeline Object Architecture (TOA) that uses graph-guided LLM extraction to synthesize a concise timeline of dedup
