## 摘要

Deep reinforcement learning has demonstrated remarkable success across various domains. However, the tight coupling between training and inference processes makes accelerating DRL training an essential challenge for DRL optimization. Two key issues hinder efficient DRL training: (1) the significant variation in computational intensity across different DRL algorithms and even among operations within the same algorithm complicates hardware platform selection, while (2) DRL's wide dynamic range could lead to substantial reward errors with conventional FP16+FP32 mixed-precision quantization.
