## 摘要

Merging separately trained LoRA adapters is a practical alternative to joint multi-task training, but it often hurts performance. Existing methods usually treat the LoRA update $ΔW = BA$ as a single object and do not distinguish the two LoRA matrices. We show that the main source of LoRA merge interference comes from the output-side matrix $B$. Across tasks, $B$ repeatedly uses a small set of shared directions, while $A$ remains much more task-specific.
