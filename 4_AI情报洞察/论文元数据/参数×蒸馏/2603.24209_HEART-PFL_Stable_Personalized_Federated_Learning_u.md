## 摘要

Personalized Federated Learning (PFL) aims to deliver effective client-specific models under heterogeneous distributions, yet existing methods suffer from shallow prototype alignment and brittle server-side distillation. We propose HEART-PFL, a dual-sided framework that (i) performs depth-aware Hierarchical Directional Alignment (HDA) using cosine similarity in the early stage and MSE matching in the deep stage to preserve client specificity, and (ii) stabilizes global updates through Adversarial Knowledge Transfer (AKT) with symmetric KL distillation on clean and adversarial proxy data.
