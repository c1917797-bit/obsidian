## 摘要

The key-value (KV) cache is widely treated as essential state in transformer inference, and a large body of work engineers policies to compress, evict, or approximate its entries. We prove that this state is entirely redundant: keys and values at every layer are deterministic projections of the residual stream, and recomputing them from a single residual vector per token incurs exactly zero reconstruction error, not approximately, but bit-identically.
