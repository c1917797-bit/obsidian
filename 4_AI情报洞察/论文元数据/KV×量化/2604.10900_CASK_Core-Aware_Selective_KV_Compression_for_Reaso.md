## 摘要

In large language models performing long-form reasoning, the KV cache grows rapidly with decode length, creating bottlenecks in memory and inference stability. Existing reasoning-oriented KV compression has mostly followed an eviction-centered view: estimate token importance more accurately, then discard lower-ranked entries. Our analysis suggests that scorer refinement alone often fails to substantially reorganize the actual keep-set and may therefore not be the main lever for preserving reasoning behavior.
