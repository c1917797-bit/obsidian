Federated Learning (FL) enables collaborative model training across distributed clients without sharing local data, thus reducing privacy risks in decentralized systems.  However, the exposure of gradients during training can lead to significant privacy leakage, particularly under gradient inversion attacks.  To address this issue, we propose Federated Error Minimization (FedEM), an input-level defense framework that injects learnable perturbations into client data and jointly optimizes both the model and the perturbation generator.
