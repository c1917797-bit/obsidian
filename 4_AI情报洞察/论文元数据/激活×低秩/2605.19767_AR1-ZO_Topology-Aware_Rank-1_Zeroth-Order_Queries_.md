## 摘要

Zeroth-order (ZO) optimization enables large-language-model fine-tuning without storing backpropagation activations, while LoRA supplies compact trainable adapters. Combining them creates a rank paradox: increasing LoRA rank improves adapter capacity, but standard two-point ZO either perturbs a rank-dependent number of coordinates or, under atomwise updates, can make the finite-difference signal unobservable.
