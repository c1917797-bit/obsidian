## 摘要

Hybrid State-Space models combine Attention with recurrent State-Space Model (SSM) layers, balancing eidetic memory from Attention with compressed fading memory from SSMs. This yields smaller Key-Value caches and faster decoding than Transformers, along with a richer architectural design space. Exploring that design space at scale has so far required training from scratch, a barrier that has kept most large-model Hybrid research within a narrow range of architectures. We introduce Priming, a method that turns Hybrid architecture design from a pre-training problem into a knowledge transfer one.
