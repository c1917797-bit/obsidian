## 摘要

We present FDM (Fan Duality Model), a linear sequence architecture that resolves the fundamental tension between memory efficiency and associative recall in sequence modeling. FDM separates sequence processing into two components: a wave component (recurrent scan via phase-preserving Givens rotations) that compresses long-range patterns into a fixed-size complex hidden state, and a particle component (local-global cache) that retrieves specific tokens via learned associative addressing with W+K=272 slots independent of sequence length N.
