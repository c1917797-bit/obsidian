## 摘要

Federated learning (FL) enables training of a global model while keeping raw data on end-devices. Despite this, FL has shown to leak private user information and thus in practice, it is often coupled with methods such as differential privacy (DP) and secure vector sum to provide formal privacy guarantees to its participants. In realistic cross-device deployments, the data are highly heterogeneous, so vanilla federated learning converges slowly and generalizes poorly.
