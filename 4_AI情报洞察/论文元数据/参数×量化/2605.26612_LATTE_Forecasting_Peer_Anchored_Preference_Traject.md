## 摘要

Personalized generation with frozen large language models requires a conditioning signal that is both compact and current. Existing personalization methods typically retrieve or summarize user histories in text, or compress them into static latent profiles and soft prompts. These approaches are efficient, but they treat a user's past behavior as an aggregate profile and therefore mix stable identity, recent drift, and item content in the same representation. We propose LAtent Trajectory Tracking and Extrapolation (LATTE), a framework that represents personalization as forecasting a peer anchor
