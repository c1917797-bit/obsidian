## 摘要

A fundamental tension arises when accelerating diffusion-based generative models via their deterministic probability flow ordinary differential equation (PF-ODE)  paths, which we formally identify as the *contractivity trap*: efficient inference requires large step sizes, but stable convergence demands strong contractivity that limits model expressiveness. This results in error accumulation in inference as contractivity weakens. In this work, we propose a principled inference approach, called *SteinDiff*, that relaxes the contractivity constraints through reference-free Stein regularization. S
