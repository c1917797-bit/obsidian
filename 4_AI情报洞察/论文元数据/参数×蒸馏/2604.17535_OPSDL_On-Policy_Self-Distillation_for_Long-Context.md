## 摘要

Extending the effective context length of large language models (LLMs) remains a central challenge for real-world applications. While recent post-training methods have made progress in long-context scaling, they either rely on high-quality supervision data or sparse sequence-level rewards, leading to unstable and inefficient optimization.
