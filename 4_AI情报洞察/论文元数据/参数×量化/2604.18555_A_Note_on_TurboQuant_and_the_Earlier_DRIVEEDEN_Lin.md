## 摘要

This note clarifies the relationship between the recent TurboQuant work and the earlier DRIVE (NeurIPS 2021) and EDEN (ICML 2022) schemes. DRIVE is a 1-bit quantizer that EDEN extended to any $b&gt;0$ bits per coordinate; we refer to them collectively as EDEN.   First, TurboQuant$_{\text{mse}}$ is a special case of EDEN obtained by fixing EDEN's scalar scale parameter to $S=1$.
