## 摘要

Reasoning models spend different amounts of useful computation across instances, but it remains unclear when a learned stopping rule improves over simple confidence or convergence thresholds. We study this question with LearnStop, a hidden-state-free checkpoint stopper for reasoning language models. At fixed budget checkpoints, LearnStop probes a short answer from the current reasoning prefix and predicts prefix correctness from online features such as answer confidence, entropy, prefix vote share, answer stability, and backtracking-marker density.
