## 摘要

Recent phone-class mobile SoCs expose practical NPU execution paths for on-device vision-language model (VLM) inference, but developers still lack phase-level guidance for mapping VLM pipelines across heterogeneous backends. We present a hardware-in-the-loop characterization of VLM inference on the Qualcomm SM8750 (Snapdragon 8 Elite), covering phase throughput, cache-state effects, 100-run thermal stability, energy, heterogeneous CPU/NPU pipeline configurations, and visual-token-budget sensitivity.
