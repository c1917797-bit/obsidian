## 摘要

Federated low-rank adaptation methods are attractive for fine-tuning large models under communication and privacy constraints, but heterogeneous client data can induce geometric misalignment between local low-rank updates. We study whether this subspace misalignment leads to destructive aggregation and slower convergence in LoRA-based federated learning. We propose a subspace-regularized federated LoRA objective that encourages local client updates to remain close to a shared global reference subspace. We present a complete empirical evaluation on two pretrained models, RoBERTa-large and SmolL
