## 摘要

AI text detectors amplify a pretrained typicality axis; they do not construct an AI-vs-human boundary. On raw encoders before any task supervision, projecting onto centroid(AI)-centroid(HC3) achieves NYT-vs-HC3 AUROC 0.806/0.944/0.834 across three architectures (86-106% of the fine-tuned discrimination ceiling: on RoBERTa-base, raw projection exceeds fine-tuning); on RoBERTa-base, full fine-tuning reduces discrimination below raw on both fluent-formal populations tested.
