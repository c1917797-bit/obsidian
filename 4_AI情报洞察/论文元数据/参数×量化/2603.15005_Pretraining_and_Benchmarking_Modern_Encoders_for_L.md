Encoder-only transformers remain essential for practical NLP tasks. While recent advances in multilingual models have improved cross-lingual capabilities, low-resource languages such as Latvian remain underrepresented in pretraining corpora, and few monolingual Latvian encoders currently exist. We address this gap by pretraining a suite of Latvian-specific encoders based on RoBERTa, DeBERTaV3, and ModernBERT architectures, including long-context variants, and evaluating them across a diverse set of Latvian diagnostic and linguistic benchmarks.
