## 摘要

We train nine sparse autoencoders (SAEs) on the residual stream of Qwen 3.5-35B-A3B, a 35-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with a hybrid GatedDeltaNet/attention architecture, and use them to identify and steer five agentic behavioral traits. Our method trains linear probes on SAE latent activations, then projects the probe weights back through the SAE decoder to obtain continuous steering vectors in the model's native activation space.
