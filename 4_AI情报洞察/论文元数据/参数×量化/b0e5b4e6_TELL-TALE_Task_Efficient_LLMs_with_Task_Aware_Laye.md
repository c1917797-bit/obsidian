## 摘要

In this paper we introduce Tale, Task-Aware Layer Elimination, an inference-time algorithm that prunes entire transformer layers in an LLM by directly optimizing task-specific validation performance. We evaluate Tale on 9 tasks and 5 models, including LLaMA 3.1 8B, Qwen 2.5 7B, Qwen 2.5 0.5B, Mistral 7B, and Lucie 7B, under both zero-shot and few-shot settings. Unlike prior approaches, Tale requires no retraining and consistently improves accuracy while reducing computational cost across all benchmarks. Furthermore, applying Tale during finetuning leads to additional performance gains. Finally
