## 摘要

Time-series forecasting presents a persistent trade-off between simple, scalable linear models that struggle with complex dynamics and large neural architectures that offer high accuracy at a steep computational cost. We introduce RAYQUAZA, a parameter-efficient architecture designed to fill this gap. RAYQUAZA learns an adaptive basis decomposition of the signal into three complementary components: a smooth trend extractor, a residual correction branch, and a novel input-conditioned radial basis function layer.
