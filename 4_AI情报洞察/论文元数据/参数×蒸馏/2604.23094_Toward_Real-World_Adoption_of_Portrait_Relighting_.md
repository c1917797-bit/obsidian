We address these challenges with Hybrid Domain Knowledge Fusion, a paradigm that fuses the specialized strengths of synthetic, One-Light-at-A-Time (OLAT), and real-world datasets into a compact model. Our approach features specialized prior models hardened by domain-aware adaptation, followed by augmented knowledge distillation into a lightweight student model with multi-domain expertise.
