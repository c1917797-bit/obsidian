## 摘要

Combining attention's global retrieval with the sequential importance signal of state space models (SSMs) is the open challenge of hybrid language modeling. Transformers see everywhere but cannot prioritize; SSMs know what matters but cannot revisit. Existing hybrids -- Jamba (block level) and Hymba (head level) -- place the two in separate compartments, so neither informs the other during the attention computation itself. We propose SISA (SSM-Informed Softmax Attention), which adds an SSM-derived importance term directly inside the attention score and realizes the full operation as a single S
