## 摘要

We develop tractable convex relaxations for rank-constrained quadratic optimization problems over $n \times m$ matrices, a setting for which tractable relaxations are typically only available when the objective or constraints admit spectral structure. We derive lifted semidefinite relaxations that do not require such spectral terms.
