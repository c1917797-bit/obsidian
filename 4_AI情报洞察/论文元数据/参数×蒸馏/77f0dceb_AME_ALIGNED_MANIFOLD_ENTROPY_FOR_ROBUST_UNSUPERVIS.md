## 摘要

Unsupervised knowledge distillation is a long-established technique for knowledge transfer, and has regained attention in the emergence of pre-trained vision-language models (VLMs). However, its objectives, i.e, probability distributions, are inherently scalar and directionless, which represent a sharp contrast to the similarity-based objectives employed in vision–language model training. As a result, the unsupervised distillation paradigm fails to impose sufficient cross-modal alignment, such alignment is essential for generalization in vision–language knowledge distillation.
