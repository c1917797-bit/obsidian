## 摘要

Positron Emission Tomography (PET) image reconstruction is inherently challenged by Poisson noise and physical degradation factors, which are further exacerbated in limited-angle acquisitions. While deep learning methods demonstrate promising performance, their generalization to unseen clinical data distributions remains limited without extensive retraining. We propose PET-Adapter, a test-time domain adaptation framework for generative PET reconstruction models pretrained solely on phantom data.
