## 摘要

Scaling LLM vocabulary is often used to reduce input sequence length and alleviate attention's quadratic cost. Yet, current LLM architectures impose a critical bottleneck to this procedure: the output projection layer scales linearly with vocabulary size, rendering substantial expansion impractical. We propose SpeLLM, a method that decouples input and output vocabularies by predicting character-level strings through multiple output heads.
