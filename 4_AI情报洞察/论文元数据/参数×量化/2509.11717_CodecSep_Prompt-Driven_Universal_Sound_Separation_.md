## 摘要

Text-guided sound separation enables flexible audio editing, assistive listening, and open-domain source extraction, but systems such as AudioSep remain too expensive for low-latency edge or codec-mediated deployment. Existing neural audio codec separators are efficient, yet largely restricted to fixed stems or closed taxonomies. We introduce CodecSep, a prompt-driven universal sound separation framework that extracts sources directly in neural audio codec latent space. CodecSep combines a frozen DAC backbone with a lightweight FiLM-conditioned Transformer masker driven by CLAP text embeddings
