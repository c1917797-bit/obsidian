## 摘要

Federated Learning has become a promising framework for preserving data privacy in collaborative training across decentralized data sources. However, the presence of data heterogeneity remains a significant challenge, impacting both the performance and efficiency of FL systems. To address this, we introduce CS-pFedTM (Communication-Efficient and Similarity-based Personalized Federated Learning with Tsetlin Machine), a method that addresses this challenge by jointly enforcing communication-aware resource allocation and heterogeneity-driven personalization.
