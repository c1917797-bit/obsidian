## 摘要

Tokenizer-free language models eliminate the tokenizer step of the language modeling pipeline by operating directly on bytes; patch-based variants further aggregate contiguous byte spans into patches for efficiency. However, the average patch size chosen at the model design stage governs a tight trade-off: larger patches reduce compute and KV-cache footprint, but degrade modeling quality.
