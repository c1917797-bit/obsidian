## 摘要

Diffusion-based LLMs (dLLMs) fundamentally depart from traditional autoregressive (AR) LLM inference: they leverage bidirectional attention, block-wise KV cache refreshing, cross-step reuse, and a non-GEMM-centric sampling phase. These characteristics make current dLLMs incompatible with most existing NPUs, as their inference patterns, in particular the reduction-heavy, top-$k$-driven sampling stage, demand new ISA and memory hierarchy support beyond that of AR accelerators.
