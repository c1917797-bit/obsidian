Quantization presents a powerful approach for reducing the memory footprint and accelerating the inference of Large Language Models (LLMs). However, it faces a fundamental dilemma: computation-friendly Scalar Quantization (SQ) suffers performance degradation at ultra-low bit-widths, whereas memory-friendly Vector Quantization (VQ) maintains higher accuracy but fails to reduce computational demand.
