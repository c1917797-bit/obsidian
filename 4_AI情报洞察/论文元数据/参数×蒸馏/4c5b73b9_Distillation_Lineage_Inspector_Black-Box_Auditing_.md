## 摘要

Model distillation has emerged as a widely used technique for creating efficient models tailored to specific tasks or domains. However, its reliance on knowledge from foundation models raises significant legal concerns regarding intellectual property rights. To address this issue, we propose the Distillation Lineage Inspector (DLI) framework, which enables model developers to determine whether their large language models (LLMs) have been distilled without authorization, even in black-box settings where training data and model architecture are inaccessible.
