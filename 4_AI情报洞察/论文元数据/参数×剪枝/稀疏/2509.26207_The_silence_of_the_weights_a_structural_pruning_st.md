## 摘要

Transformer-based models have become the state of the art across multiple domains, from natural language processing to machine listening, thanks to the attention mechanisms. However, the attention layers require a large number of parameters and high-end hardware for both training and inference. We propose a novel channel-pruning technique explicitly targeted at the attention mechanism, decoupling the pruning of each head and the four layers in the attention block: query, key, value, and output projection matrices, employing a second-order metric to score the network's parameters.
