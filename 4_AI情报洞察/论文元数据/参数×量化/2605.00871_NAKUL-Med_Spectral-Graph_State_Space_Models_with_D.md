## 摘要

State space models (SSMs) achieve linear-time complexity but struggle with multi-channel physiological signals due to three limitations: fixed kernels cannot capture multi-scale temporal dynamics (motor preparation over hundreds of milliseconds vs. execution transients in tens of milliseconds), Markovian state updates restrict global context for periodic oscillations, and channel-independent processing ignores spatial electrode topology.
