## 摘要

Post-training quantization (PTQ) is one of the most practical ways to reduce the serving cost of Large Language Models (LLMs), but activation quantization remains difficult because outlier-dominated channels lead to large quantization errors. This paper investigates whether part of this degradation is caused by over-migration in scaling-based equivalent transformations. We introduce a quantile-robust scaling policy for SmoothRot-style transforms by replacing max-based activation statistics with high quantiles, and we complement it with constrained gradient-based optimization of channel scales.
