## 摘要

Node classification on text-attributed graphs (TAGs) is a fundamental task with broad applications in citation analysis, social networks, and recommendation systems. Current GNN-based approaches suffer from shallow text encoding and heavy dependence on labeled data, limiting their effectiveness in label-scarce settings. While large language models (LLMs) naturally address the text understanding gap with deep semantic reasoning, existing LLM-for-graph methods either still require abundant labels during training or fail to exploit the rich structural signals freely available in graph topology.
