## 摘要

Existing research of 3D LLMs still struggles to achieve efficient and explainable reasoning, primarily due to the under-exploration of the mechanism of human-like scene-object grounded reasoning. This paper bridges the gap by presenting a novel framework. We first introduce a Chain-of-Thought reasoning framework in 3D scenes (SceneCOT), decoupling a complex reasoning task into simpler and manageable problems, and building corresponding visual clues based on multimodal expert modules. To enable such a framework, we build the first large-scale 3D scene Chain-of-Thought reasoning dataset, SceneCO
