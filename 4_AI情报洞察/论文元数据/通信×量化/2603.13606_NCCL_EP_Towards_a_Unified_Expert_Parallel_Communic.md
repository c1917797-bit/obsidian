## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures have become essential for scaling large language models, driving the development of specialized device-initiated communication libraries such as DeepEP, Hybrid-EP, and others. These libraries demonstrate the performance benefits of GPU-initiated RDMA for MoE dispatch and combine operations.   This paper presents NCCL EP (Expert Parallelism), a ground-up MoE communication library built entirely on NCCL's Device API.
