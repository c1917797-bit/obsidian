## 摘要

As context windows in LLMs scale to 100K+ tokens, the key-value (KV) cache becomes the dominant memory bottleneck, with recent methods claiming 80-90% savings and minimal benchmark degradation. We argue these evaluations miss a structural issue: attention is not just storage but routing, and retaining KV pairs does not guarantee semantic accessibility. We propose a physics-inspired view of KV compression as a controlled perturbation of token-level routing, distinguishing retention, accessibility, and utilization.
