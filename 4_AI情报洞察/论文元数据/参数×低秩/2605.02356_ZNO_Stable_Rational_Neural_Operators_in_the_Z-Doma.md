## 摘要

We introduce the Z-Domain Neural Operator (ZNO), a causal neural operator whose layers are stable low-rank multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) rational filters parameterized directly in the $z$-plane. ZNO addresses a limitation of existing operator learning methods, many of which are primarily tailored for continuous-time problems, while a large class of system-identification problems is intrinsically discrete-time.
