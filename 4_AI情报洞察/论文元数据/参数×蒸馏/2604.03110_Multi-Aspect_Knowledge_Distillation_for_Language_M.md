## 摘要

Knowledge distillation is an effective technique for pre-trained language model compression. However, existing methods only focus on the knowledge distribution among layers, which may cause the loss of fine-grained information in the alignment process. To address this issue, we introduce the Multi-aspect Knowledge Distillation (MaKD) method, which mimics the self-attention and feed-forward modules in greater depth to capture rich language knowledge information at different aspects.
