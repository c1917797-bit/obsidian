## 摘要

We propose a new optimization method, the Nyström-enhanced relaxed scalar auxiliary variable method (N-RSAV), which incorporates curvature information into the RSAV framework to accelerate convergence while preserving an unconditional modified energy dissipation law. Existing RSAV-based methods rely solely on first-order information and often suffer from slow convergence, particularly for ill-conditioned problems such as those arising in physics-informed neural networks (PINNs).
