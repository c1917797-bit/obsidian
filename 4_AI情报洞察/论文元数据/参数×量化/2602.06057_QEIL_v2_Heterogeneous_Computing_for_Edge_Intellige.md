Our prior QEIL v1 (Kumar &amp; Jha, 2026) achieved 4.82x IPW improvement but relied on static efficiency factors, greedy optimization, and unverified candidate selection. QEIL v2 replaces every static heuristic with physics-grounded, runtime-adaptive models.
