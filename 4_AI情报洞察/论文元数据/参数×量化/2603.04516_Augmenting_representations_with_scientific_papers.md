## 摘要

Astronomers have acquired vast repositories of multimodal data, including images, spectra, and time series, complemented by decades of literature that analyzes astrophysical sources. Still, these data sources are rarely systematically integrated. This work introduces a contrastive learning framework designed to align X-ray spectra with domain knowledge extracted from scientific literature, facilitating the development of shared multimodal representations. Establishing this connection is inherently complex, as scientific texts encompass a broader and more diverse physical context than spectra.
