## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) perform internal computations in continuous vector spaces yet produce discrete tokens -- a fundamental mismatch whose geometric consequences remain poorly understood. We develop a mathematical framework that interprets LLM hidden states as points on a latent semantic manifold: a Riemannian submanifold equipped with the Fisher information metric, where tokens correspond to Voronoi regions partitioning the manifold.
