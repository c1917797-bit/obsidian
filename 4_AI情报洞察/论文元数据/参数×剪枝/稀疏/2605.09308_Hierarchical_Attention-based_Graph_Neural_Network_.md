## 摘要

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) excel at relational reasoning but face two persistent challenges: the lack of interpretable attribution for heterogeneous node types, and the computational overhead of message passing over large, noisy graphs. We propose the Hierarchical Attention-based Heterogeneous GNN (HA-HeteroGNN), a framework that addresses both issues through a unied explainability-to-pruning pipeline.
