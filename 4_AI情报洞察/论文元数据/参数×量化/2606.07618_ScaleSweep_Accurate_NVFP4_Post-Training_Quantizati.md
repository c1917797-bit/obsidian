## 摘要

NVFP4 is a recently introduced hardware-supported FP4 format that improves the fidelity of 4-bit quantization through fine-grained block scales. However, existing NVFP4 scale initialization methods still primarily rely on AbsMax initialization, which leaves a noticeable gap to the optimal solution. To address this, we propose ScaleSweep, a simple and efficient scale optimization method that sweeps over feasible block scale candidates and selects the candidate that minimizes a target objective. We further provide a theoretical analysis of NVFP4 quantization and derive both lower and upper bound
