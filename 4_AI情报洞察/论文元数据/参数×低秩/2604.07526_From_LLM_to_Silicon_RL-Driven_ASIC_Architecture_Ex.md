## 摘要

We present an RL-driven compiler that jointly optimizes ASIC architecture, memory hierarchy, and workload partitioning for AI inference across 3nm to 28nm. The design space is formulated as a single Markov Decision Process with mixed discrete-continuous actions and a unified Power-Performance-Area (PPA) objective. Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) with Mixture-of-Experts gating explores the joint space of mesh topology, per-core microarchitecture, and operator placement.
