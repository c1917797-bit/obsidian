## 摘要

Reasoning models think in long, unstructured streams with no mechanism for compressing or organizing their own intermediate state. We introduce MEMENTO: a method that teaches models to segment reasoning into blocks, compress each block into a memento, i.e., a dense state summary, and reason forward by attending only to mementos, reducing context, KV cache, and compute.
