## 摘要

We present GIFT: a Gradient-aware Immunization technique to defend diffusion models against malicious Fine-Tuning while preserving their ability to generate safe content. Existing safety methods, such as safety checkers, are easily bypassed, and concept erasure methods fail under adversarial fine-tuning. GIFT addresses this by framing immunization as a bi-level optimization problem: the upper-level objective degrades the model’s ability to represent malicious concepts using representation noising and maximization, while the lower-level objective preserves performance on safe data.
