## 摘要

Time series forecasting under limited data remains challenging due to model overfitting and insufficient structural regularization. In this work, we uncover a sparsity-oriented scaling phenomenon: as training data increases, model parameters naturally become sparser—even in simple linear models. This observation motivates the introduction of learned sparsity as an effective prior to improve model generalization under data-scarce regimes.
