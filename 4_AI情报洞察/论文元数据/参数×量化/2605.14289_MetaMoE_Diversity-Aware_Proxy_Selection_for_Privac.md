## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models scale capacity by combining specialized experts, but most existing approaches assume centralized access to training data. In practice, data are distributed across clients and cannot be shared due to privacy constraints, making unified MoE training challenging. We propose MetaMoE, a privacy-preserving framework that unifies independently trained, domain-specialized experts into a single MoE using public proxy data as surrogates for inaccessible private data.
