## 摘要

We introduce a Curriculum-Based Termination Critic (CBTC) for hierarchical reinforcement learning (HRL) to solve the problem of program decomposition for scaleable programming in complex task environments. Traditional termination critics yet make some static heuristics on the other side that have difficulties to cope with different tasks in complexity and prevents the agent to learn right hierarchy abstractions effectively. The CBTC presents a dynamic curriculum-driven framework that selects the difficulty of the tasks on the fly and incrementally adjusts the difficulty according to the agent'
