## 摘要

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has become a popular representation for 3D avatar modeling due to its fast training and real-time rendering. However, the state-of-the-art methods struggle to generalize from sparse inputs and often fail to recover realistic geometry. We introduce a motion-aware surface smoothing framework to improve 3DGS for learning from monocular human videos. Our method regularizes the training of Gaussian parameters, modulates the Adaptive Density Control (ADC) for improving surface quality, and supervises Gaussian motions under unseen camera viewpoints.
