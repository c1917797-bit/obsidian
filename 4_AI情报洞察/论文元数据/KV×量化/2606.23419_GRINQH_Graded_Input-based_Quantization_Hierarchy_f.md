## 摘要

Autoregressive decoding with LLMs is primarily bottlenecked by GPU memory bandwidth, especially in edge-computing settings. While quantization is essential for mitigating this bottleneck, most existing methods treat inference as a uniform process and fail to account for the asymmetry between the compute-bound prefill stage and the memory-bound decoding stage. We propose GRINQH (GRaded INput-based Quantization Hierarchy), a weight-only post-training quantization framework that accelerates decoding by unifying quantization and sparsification.
