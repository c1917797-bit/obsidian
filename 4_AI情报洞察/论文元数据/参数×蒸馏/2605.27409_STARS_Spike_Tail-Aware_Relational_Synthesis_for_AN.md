## 摘要

SNNs promise energy-efficient and low-latency inference, but their performance still trails that of ANNs. ANN-to-SNN knowledge distillation helps narrow this gap, yet the original training data are often unavailable in practical deployment settings. Existing data-free knowledge distillation (DFKD) methods synthesize surrogate data by matching teacher-side priors, especially BN statistics, but these ANN-oriented constraints mainly regularize mean and variance and therefore remain under-constrained for SNN students whose responses depend on threshold-crossing dynamics.
