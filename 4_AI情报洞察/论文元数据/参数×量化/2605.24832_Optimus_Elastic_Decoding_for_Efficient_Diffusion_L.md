## 摘要

Large language model (LLM) serving is fundamentally limited by inefficient hardware utilization. Autoregressive (AR) decoding underutilizes GPUs due to its strictly sequential execution, while diffusion LLMs (DLLMs) improve throughput by decoding multiple tokens per iteration. However, fixed block-size diffusion decoding exhibits strong load sensitivity: large blocks exploit idle GPU resources under low load, but saturate early and incur substantial redundant computation under high load.
