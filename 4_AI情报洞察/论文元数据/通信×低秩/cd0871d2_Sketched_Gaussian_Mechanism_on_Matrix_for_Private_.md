## 摘要

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), which modifies frozen pre-trained parameters via the product of two trainable low-rank factors, has been widely adopted for communication-efficient fine-tuning of language models, including extensions to federated learning (FL). Nevertheless, two challenges arise at scale: (i) for very large models, the adapter factors can remain high-dimensional, leading to nontrivial communication costs between clients and the server; and (ii) transmitting local adapters between clients and the server risks privacy leakage.
