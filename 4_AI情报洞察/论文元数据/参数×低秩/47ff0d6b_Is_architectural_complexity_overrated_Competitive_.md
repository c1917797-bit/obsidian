## 摘要

We introduce RELATE, a minimal, fully real–valued and interpretable KGE whose phase–modulus decomposition cleanly separates directional semantics (a smooth sinusoidal phase for (anti-)symmetry and composition) from magnitude/type semantics (a slope–weighted modulus with a lightweight type bias). We prove full expressivity for finite KGs and establish invariance/identifiability and training–stability
