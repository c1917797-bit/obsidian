## 摘要

Federated fine-tuning of multimodal large language models faces significant challenges in communication costs, which can be addressed by Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA). Existing methods typically allow all clients to collaboratively learn and share a single LoRA adapter. However, we identify a long-overlooked issue: under non-IID data, federated fine-tuning can even underperform standalone local training ("$1+1<1$").
