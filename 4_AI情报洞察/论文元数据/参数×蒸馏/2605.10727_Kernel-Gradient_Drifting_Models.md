## 摘要

We propose kernel-gradient drifting, a one-step generative modeling framework that replaces the fixed Euclidean displacement direction in drifting models with directions induced by the kernel itself. Standard drifting is attractive because it enables fast, high-quality generation without distilling a large pretrained diffusion model, but its theory is currently understood mainly for Gaussian kernels, where the drift coincides with smoothed score matching and is identifiable.
