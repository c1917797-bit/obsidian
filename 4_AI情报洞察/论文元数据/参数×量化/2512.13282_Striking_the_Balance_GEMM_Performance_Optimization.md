## 摘要

The high computational and memory demands of modern deep learning (DL) workloads have led to the development of specialized hardware devices from cloud to edge, such as AMD's Ryzen AI XDNA NPUs. Optimizing general matrix multiplication (GEMM) algorithms for these architectures is critical for improving DL workload performance. To this end, this paper presents a common systematic methodology to optimize GEMM workloads across the two current NPU generations, namely XDNA and XDNA2. Our implementations exploit the unique architectural features of AMD's NPUs and address key performance bottlenecks
