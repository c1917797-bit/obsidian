## 摘要

Recent alignment work on Large Language Models (LLMs) suggests preference optimization can improve reasoning by shifting probability mass toward better solutions. We test this claim in a resource-constrained setting by applying GRPO with LoRA to SLMs (up to 3B) for math reasoning on GSM8K and MATH datasets with difficulty-stratified analyses. As problem difficulty increases, accuracy plateaus, revealing a capacity boundary: GRPO primarily reshapes output preferences without reliably improving hardest-tier solving.
