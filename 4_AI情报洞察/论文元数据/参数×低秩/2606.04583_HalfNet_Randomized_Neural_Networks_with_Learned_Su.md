## 摘要

Many researchers investigated neural networks with some of their weights fixed to values randomly drawn from a given distribution, e.g., $N(0, I)$. Our proposed HalfNet draws random weights from $N(0, Σ)$, where $Σ$, which defines the geometry of the distribution, has a low-rank factorization that we learn from data. Experiments on MNIST and CIFAR-10 demonstrate that HalfNet can match the performance of fully trained multilayer perceptrons while using substantially fewer parameters.
