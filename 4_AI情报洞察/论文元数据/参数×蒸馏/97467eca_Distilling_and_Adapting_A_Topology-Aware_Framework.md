Multiplex Biological Networks (MBNs), which represent multiple interaction types between entities, are crucial for understanding complex biological systems. Yet, existing methods often inadequately model multiplexity, struggle to integrate structural and sequence information, and face difficulties in zero-shot prediction for unseen entities with no prior neighbourhood information.  To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework for zero-shot interaction prediction in MBNs by leveraging context-aware representation learning and knowledge distillation.
