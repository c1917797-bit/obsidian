## 摘要

Automated red-teaming has emerged as an essential approach for identifying vulnerabilities in large language models (LLMs). However, most existing methods rely on fixed attack templates and focus primarily on individual high-severity flaws,limiting their adaptability to evolving defenses and their ability to detect complex, high-exploitability vulnerabilities.
