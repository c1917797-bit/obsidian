## 摘要

However, prevailing cascaded pipelines propagate transcription errors and end‐to‐end models lack the temporal granularity required for reliable frame‐level detection. To address these limitations, we introduce DEnSNEL (Dense End‐to‐end Spoken Named Entity Localizer), the first end-to-end model to perform direct, frame-level spoken named entity localization, without intermediate character or text representations.
