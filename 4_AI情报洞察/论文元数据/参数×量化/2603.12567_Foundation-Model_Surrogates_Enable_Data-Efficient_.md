However, current AL relies predominantly on Gaussian Process (GP) and Random Forest (RF) surrogates, which suffer from complementary limitations: GP underfits complex composition-property landscapes due to rigid kernel assumptions, while RF produces unreliable heuristic uncertainty estimates in small-data regimes.
