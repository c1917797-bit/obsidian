## 摘要

Binary Neural Networks (BNNs) are receiving growing interest for enabling energy-intensive deep learning on resource-limited edge devices. Traditionally, the training methods of such models rely on minimizing the quantization error in forward propagation and approximating the sign function in full-precision models. However, such methods do not leverage the nature of training in the space of binary weights. To address this issue, we propose a latent-free method called Kernel Binary Optimizer (KBOP) to enable binary deep learning. The proposal is largely based on three major technical innovation
