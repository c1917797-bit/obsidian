## 摘要

We extract 21-emotion vector sets from twelve small language models (six architectures x base/instruct, 1B-8B parameters) under a unified comprehension-mode pipeline at fp16 precision, and compare the resulting geometries via representational similarity analysis on raw cosine RDMs. The five mature architectures (Qwen 2.5 1.5B, SmolLM2 1.7B, Llama 3.2 3B, Mistral 7B v0.3, Llama 3.1 8B) share nearly identical 21-emotion geometry, with pairwise RDM Spearman correlations of 0.74-0.92.
