## 摘要

Structured pruning and knowledge distillation (KD) are typical techniques for compressing large language models, but it remains unclear how they should be applied at pretraining scale, especially to recent mixture-of-experts (MoE) models. In this work, we systematically study MoE compression in large-scale pretraining, focusing on three key questions: whether pruning provides a better initialization than training from scratch, how expert compression choices affect the final model after continued training, and which training strategy is most effective.
