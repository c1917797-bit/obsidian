## 摘要

We study matrix and tensor denoising when the underlying signal is \textbf{not} necessarily low-rank. In the tensor setting, we observe \[ Y = X^\ast + Z \in \mathbb{R}^{p_1 \times p_2 \times p_3}, \] where $X^\ast$ is an unknown signal tensor and $Z$ is a noise tensor. We propose a one-step variant of the higher-order SVD (HOSVD) estimator, denoted $\widetilde X$, and show that, uniformly over any user-specified Tucker ranks $(r_1,r_2,r_3)$, with high probability, \[ \|\widetilde X - X^\ast\|_{\mathrm F}^2 = O\Big( κ^2\Big\{r_1r_2r_3 + \sum_{k=1}^3 p_k r_k\Big\} + ξ_{(r_1,r_2,r_3)}^2 \Big). \
