## 摘要

Decentralized training introduces critical security risks when executed across untrusted, geographically distributed nodes. While existing Byzantine-tolerant literature addresses data parallel (DP) training through robust aggregation methods, pipeline parallelism (PP) presents fundamentally distinct challenges. In PP, model layers are distributed across workers where the activations and their gradients flow between stages rather than being aggregated, making traditional DP approaches inapplicable. We propose SENTINEL, a verification mechanism for PP training *without computation duplication*.
