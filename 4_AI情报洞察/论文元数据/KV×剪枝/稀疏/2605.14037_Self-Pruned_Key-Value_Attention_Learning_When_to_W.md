## 摘要

Under modern test-time compute and agentic paradigms, language models process ever-longer sequences. Efficient text generation with transformer architectures is increasingly constrained by the Key-Value cache memory footprint and bandwidth. To address this limitation, we introduce Self-Pruned Key-Value Attention (SP-KV), a mechanism designed to predict future KV utility in order to reduce the size of the long-term KV cache.
