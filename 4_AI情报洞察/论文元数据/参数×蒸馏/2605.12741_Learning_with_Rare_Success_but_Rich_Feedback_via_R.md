## 摘要

Enabling Large Language Models (LLMs) to continuously improve from environmental interactions is a central challenge in post-training. While on-policy self-distillation offers a promising paradigm, existing methods predominantly treat environmental feedback as a passive conditioning signal. Consequently, they heavily rely on successful demonstrations and struggle to learn in rare-success regimes. To bridge this gap, we introduce Reflection-Enhanced Self-Distillation (RESD), a framework that transforms raw failure feedback into an active source of corrective supervision.
