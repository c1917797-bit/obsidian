## 摘要

Differentially private (DP) training of neural networks is often hindered by the large amount of noise required by gradient-based methods such as DP-SGD, which repeatedly inject high-dimensional noise in parameter space throughout training. In this paper, we propose a new framework for DP learning that avoids iterative optimization in parameter space. Instead of updating the target model using privatized gradients, we employ a hypernetwork trained on public datasets to map a private dataset to the parameters of the target model.
