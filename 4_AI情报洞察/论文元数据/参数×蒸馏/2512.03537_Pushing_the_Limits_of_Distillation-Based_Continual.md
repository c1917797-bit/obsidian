Distillation-based methods are appealing for retaining past knowledge in a shared single-model framework with low storage overhead. However, they remain constrained by the stability-plasticity dilemma: knowledge acquisition and preservation are still optimized through coupled objectives, and existing enhancement methods do not alter this underlying bottleneck.
