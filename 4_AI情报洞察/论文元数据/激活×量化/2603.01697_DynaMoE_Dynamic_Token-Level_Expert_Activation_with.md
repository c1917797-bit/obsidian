## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures have emerged as a powerful paradigm for scaling neural networks while maintaining computational efficiency. However, standard MoE implementations rely on two rigid design assumptions: (1) fixed Top-K routing where exactly K experts are activated per token, and (2) uniform expert allocation across all layers. This paper introduces DynaMoE, a novel MoE framework that relaxes both constraints through dynamic token-level expert activation and layer-wise adaptive capacity allocation.
