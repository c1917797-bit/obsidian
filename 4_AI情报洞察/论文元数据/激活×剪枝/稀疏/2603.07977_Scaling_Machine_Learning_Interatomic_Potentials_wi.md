## 摘要

Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) enable accurate large-scale atomistic simulations, yet improving their expressive capacity efficiently remains challenging. Here we systematically develop Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and Mixture-of-Linear-Experts (MoLE) architectures for MLIPs and analyze the effects of routing strategies and expert designs. We show that sparse activation combined with shared experts yields substantial performance gains, and that nonlinear MoE formulations outperform MoLE when shared experts are present, underscoring the importance of nonlinear expert specialization
