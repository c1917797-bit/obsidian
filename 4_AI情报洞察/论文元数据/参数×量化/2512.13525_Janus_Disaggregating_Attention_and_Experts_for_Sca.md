## 摘要

Serving large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models is challenging because of their large memory footprints, heterogeneous resource demands, and highly dynamic inference workloads. Most existing MoE inference systems deploy the entire model as a monolithic unit, forcing attention and MoE layers to share the same resource configuration despite their different scaling behaviors and resource bottlenecks. Such coarse-grained provisioning leads to resource inefficiency and suboptimal performance. We present JANUS, a scalable and resource-efficient MoE inference system built around three key principles.
