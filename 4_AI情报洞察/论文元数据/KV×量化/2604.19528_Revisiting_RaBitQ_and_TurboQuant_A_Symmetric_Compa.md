## 摘要

This technical note revisits the relationship between RaBitQ and TurboQuant under a unified comparison framework. We compare the two methods in terms of methodology, theoretical guarantees, and empirical performance, using a reproducible, transparent, and symmetric setup. Our results show that, despite the claimed advantage of TurboQuant, TurboQuant performs worse than RaBitQ in most tested settings of inner-product estimation, nearest-neighbor search and KV cache quantization.
