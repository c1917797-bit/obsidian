## 摘要

Continual learning (CL) remains a critical challenge when applying large language models (LLMs) to real-world situations. On the one hand, billions of parameters for LLMs add a huge computing overhead to CL. Existing techniques, on the other hand, solely address catastrophic forgetting while ignoring the possibility of knowledge transfer between tasks. Facing these challenges, we propose Attribution Scores-based Soft Orthogonality Low-Rank Adaptation (ASO-LoRA), an effective and efficient framework that simultaneously facilitates knowledge transfer while mitigating catastrophic forgetting.
