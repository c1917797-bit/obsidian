## 摘要

Rotary Positional Embedding (RoPE) is a widely adopted technique for encoding position in language models, which, while effective, causes performance breakdown when input length exceeds training length. Prior analyses assert (rightly) that long inputs cause channels to rotate ``out of distribution,'' but it is not clear how extra rotation relates to or causes pathological behavior. Through empirical and theoretical analysis we advance a unified geometric understanding of attention behavior with RoPE. We find that attention induces tight clustering of separated key and query latent point clouds
