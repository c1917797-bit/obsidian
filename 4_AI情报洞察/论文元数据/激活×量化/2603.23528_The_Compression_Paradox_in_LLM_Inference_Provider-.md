We test whether prompt compression improves inference energy efficiency in 28,421 successful API trials (28,428 planned) across three providers (OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, Anthropic Claude-3.5-Sonnet, and DeepSeek-Chat), five benchmarks (HumanEval, MBPP, GSM8K, MATH, MMLU), and four compression ratios (r in {1.0, 0.7, 0.5, 0.3}).
