## 摘要

Although tabular data are central to many real-world applications, their target distributions are often imbalanced, as the majority of samples correspond to a narrow range of values. This imbalance severely degrades performance in sparse, few-shot regions. Prior work on imbalanced regression has typically relied on coarse binning of the target space, discarding fine-grained information, or on spatial-locality assumptions inherited from image domains.
