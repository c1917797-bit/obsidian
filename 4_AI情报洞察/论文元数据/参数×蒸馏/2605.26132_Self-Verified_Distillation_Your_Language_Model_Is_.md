## 摘要

Can post-trained large language models (LLMs) further improve themselves using only unlabeled prompts, without external teachers or feedback from tools? We study this setting starting only from unlabeled seed questions with no ground-truth solutions, across three reasoning domains: math, science, and coding. We propose Self-Verified Distillation, a simple post-training refinement algorithm in which the model generates candidate solutions to these seed questions, filters them using prompt-based self-verification, and trains on the resulting self-curated dataset.
