The stable operation of off-grid photovoltaic systems requires accurate, computationally efficient solar forecasting. Contemporary deep learning models often suffer from massive computational overhead and physical blindness, generating impossible predictions. This paper introduces the Physics-Informed State Space Model (PISSM) to bridge the gap between efficiency and physical accuracy for edge-deployed microcontrollers.
