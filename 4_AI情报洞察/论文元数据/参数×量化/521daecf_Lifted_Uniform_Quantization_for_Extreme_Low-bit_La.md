## 摘要

Pushing large language models to extreme low bit-widths (e.g., 2-bit) is a critical frontier for efficient deployment, yet it presents a daunting challenge to preserving model accuracy. Current methods are trapped in a fundamental trade-off: Vector Quantization (VQ) maintains accuracy by learning expressive codebooks but is crippled by its computationally expensive, non-parallelizable lookup operations. Conversely, Uniform Quantization (UQ) is exceptionally efficient but suffers a precipitous drop in quality at such low bit-widths. To break this impasse, we propose Lifted Uniform Quantization
