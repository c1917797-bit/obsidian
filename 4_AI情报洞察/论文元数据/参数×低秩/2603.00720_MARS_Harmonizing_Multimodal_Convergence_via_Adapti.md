## 摘要

Fine-tuning Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) with parameter-efficient methods like Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) is crucial for task adaptation. However, imbalanced training dynamics across modalities often lead to suboptimal accuracy due to negative interference, a challenge typically addressed with inefficient heuristic methods such as manually tuning separate learning rates. To overcome this, we introduce MARS (Multimodal Adaptive Rank Search), an approach to discover optimal rank pairs that balance training dynamics while maximizing performance.
