Deep neural networks often contain redundant hidden units. Removing individual weights can reduce parameter count, but unstructured sparsity is not always easy to exploit in standard dense implementations. This paper develops a structured pruning framework in which complete neurons are removed using multi-armed bandit (MAB) algorithms.
