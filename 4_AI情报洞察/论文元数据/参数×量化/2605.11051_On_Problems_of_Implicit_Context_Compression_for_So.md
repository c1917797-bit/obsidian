## 摘要

LLM-based Software Engineering agents face a critical bottleneck: context length limitations cause failures on complex, long-horizon tasks. One promising solution is to encode context as continuous embeddings rather than discrete tokens, enabling denser information storage. We apply the recently proposed In-Context Autoencoder for this purpose. While the method performs well on single-shot common-knowledge and code-understanding tasks, our experiments demonstrate that it fails on multi-step agentic coding tasks.
