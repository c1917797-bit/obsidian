## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) inevitably memorize sensitive information during training, posing significant privacy risks. Machine unlearning has emerged as a promising solution to selectively remove such information without full retraining. However, existing methods are designed for dense models and overlook model sparsification, an essential technique for efficient LLM deployment. We find that unlearning effectiveness degrades substantially on sparse models.
