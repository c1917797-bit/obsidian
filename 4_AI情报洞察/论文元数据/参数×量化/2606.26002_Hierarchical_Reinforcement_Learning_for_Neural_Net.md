## 摘要

We present HiReLC, a hierarchical ensemble-reinforcement learning framework for automated joint quantization and structured pruning of deep neural networks. The framework decomposes the compression search across two levels of abstraction: low-level agents (LLAs) operate independently per block, selecting per-kernel configurations over a multi-discrete action space spanning bitwidth, pruning keep-ratio, quantization type, and granularity, while high-level agents (HLAs) coordinate global budget allocation via ensemble voting guided by Fisher Information-based sensitivity estimates.
