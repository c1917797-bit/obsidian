## 摘要

This work presents TREA, a low-precision time-multiplexed and resource-efficient edge-AI accelerator for object detection and classification, targeting stringent area-power-latency constraints of edge vision platforms. The proposed architecture integrates a dual-precision (4/8-bit) SIMD multiply-accumulate (DQ-MAC) unit based on most-significant-digit-first (MSDF) shift-and-add computation with run-time bit truncation, eliminating conventional multiplier overhead and reducing accumulator bit-width.
