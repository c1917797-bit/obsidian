## 摘要

We release Llamion, a family of 14B-parameter open-weight language models obtained by transforming Orion-14B into the standardized Llama-family architecture. The transformation is performed by Efficient Knowledge Preservation for Transformation (KEPT), a recipe that combines (i) Normal Parameter Mapping (NPM) for unchanged modules, (ii) Optimized Parameter Mapping (OPM), a training-free LayerNorm-to-RMSNorm initialization we prove optimal under the near-zero-mean activation regime induced by weight decay, and (iii) Cross-architecture Knowledge Distillation (XKD), an equal-size frozen-teacher d
