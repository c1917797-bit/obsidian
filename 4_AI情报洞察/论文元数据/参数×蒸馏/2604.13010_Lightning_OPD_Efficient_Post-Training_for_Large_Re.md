## 摘要

On-policy distillation (OPD) is an effective post-training paradigm for large language models but requires a live teacher server throughout training, resulting in substantial infrastructure overhead. We investigate whether OPD can be performed offline by precomputing teacher log-probabilities once over SFT rollouts and reusing them during training. We find that naively doing so fails to reliably match standard OPD, and trace the root cause to a previously overlooked condition we term teacher consistency, requiring that the same teacher be used for both supervised fine-tuning and OPD.
