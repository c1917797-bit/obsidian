## 摘要

In this paper, we study high-dimensional covariance matrix estimation over a network of interconnected agents, where the data are distributed and may exhibit heavy-tailed behavior. To address this challenge, we propose a new estimator that integrates the Huber loss to mitigate outliers with a non-convex regularizer to promote sparsity. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first framework that simultaneously accounts for high dimensionality, heavy tails, and distributed data in covariance estimation.
