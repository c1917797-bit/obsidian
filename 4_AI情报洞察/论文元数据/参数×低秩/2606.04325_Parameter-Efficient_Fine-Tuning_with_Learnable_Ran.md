## 摘要

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) is a popular parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) method that restricts weight updates to low-rank adapters, introducing a fixed low-rank inductive bias by optimizing in a low-dimensional subspace. In this work, we question whether a fixed-rank constraint is the most effective inductive bias for parameter-efficient fine-tuning. We introduce *Learnable Rank LoRA (LR-LoRA)*, a PEFT method in which the adapter rank is learned during the training process.
