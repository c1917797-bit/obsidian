## 摘要

Post-training quantization is widely used for compressing large neural networks, but aggressive low-bit quantization can significantly degrade model quality. A common remedy is to augment the quantized weights with a low-rank correction, leading to approximations of the form $W\approx Q+LR$. In this paper, we study this low-precision plus low-rank representation through the layer-wise reconstruction objective $\|XW-X(Q+LR)\|_F^2$, where $X$ is a calibration matrix.
