## 摘要

On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on prefixes sampled from its own policy while matching a stronger teacher. This addresses the prefix mismatch of offline distillation, but early student rollouts can still be poor, placing teacher supervision on weak or low-quality prefixes. We propose Trust-Region behavior Blending (TRB), a warmup method that replaces the early rollout policy with the closest-to-teacher behavior policy inside a student-centered KL trust region, while keeping the per-prefix reverse-KL OPD loss unchanged.
