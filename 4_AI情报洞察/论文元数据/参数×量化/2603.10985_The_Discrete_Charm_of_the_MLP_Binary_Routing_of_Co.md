## 摘要

We show that MLP layers in transformer language models perform binary routing of continuous signals: the decision of whether a token needs nonlinear processing is well-captured by binary neuron activations, even though the signals being routed are continuous. In GPT-2 Small (124M parameters), we find that specific neurons implement a consensus architecture -- seven "default-ON" neurons and one exception handler (N2123 in Layer 11) that are 93-98% mutually exclusive -- creating a binary routing switch.
