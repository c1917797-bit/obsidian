## 摘要

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), along with recent selfdistillation variants such as SDPO, evaluates each rollout against a verifier and updates the policy from that episode-level signal. However, the richer procedural information in the rollout is rarely retained or reused. Across episodes and epochs, the model repeatedly encounters related problems under a changing policy, producing cross-episode signals that episode-local updates cannot capture: which strategies consistently pass verification, which failure modes persist, which patterns recur.
