## 摘要

Self-Organizing Maps (SOM) are a classical method for unsupervised learning, vector quantization, and topographic mapping of high-dimensional data. However, existing SOM formulations often involve a trade-off between computational efficiency and a clearly defined optimization objective. Objective-based variants such as Soft Topographic Vector Quantization (STVQ) provide a principled formulation, but their neighborhood-coupled computations become expensive as the number of latent nodes increases.
