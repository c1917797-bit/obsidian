## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) are pivotal in natural language processing. The impracticality of full fine-tuning has prompted Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods like Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), optimizing low-rank matrices A and B. In distributed scenarios where privacy constraints necessitate Federated Learning (FL), however, the integration of LoRA is often unstable. Specifically, we identify that aggregating updates from multiple clients introduces statistical variance that scales with the client count, causing gradient collapse when using high-rank adapters.
