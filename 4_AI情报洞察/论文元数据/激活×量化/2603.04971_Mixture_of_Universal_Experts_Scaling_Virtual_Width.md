## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) decouples model capacity from per-token computation, yet their scalability remains limited by the physical dimensions of depth and width. To overcome this, we propose Mixture of Universal Experts (MOUE),a MoE generalization introducing a novel scaling dimension: Virtual Width. In general, MoUE aims to reuse a universal layer-agnostic expert pool across layers, converting depth into virtual width under a fixed per-token activation budget.
