## 摘要

Continual learning with vision-language models is challenged by catastrophic forgetting, where the acquisition of new knowledge compromises previously learned information. Generative replay synthesizes past samples to mitigate forgetting, while avoiding the data-privacy risks and heavy storage overhead of directly replaying historical data. However, existing methods often rely on simple class-level prompts, such as class-name with templates, resulting in synthetic images that poorly capture the semantics of original images. To address this, we propose a \textit{caption-guided replay paradigm}
