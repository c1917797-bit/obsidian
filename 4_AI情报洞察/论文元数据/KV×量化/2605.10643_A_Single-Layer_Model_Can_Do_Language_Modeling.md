## 摘要

Modern language models scale depth by stacking layers, each holding its own state - a per-layer KV cache in transformers, a per-layer matrix in Mamba, Gated DeltaNet (GDN), RWKV, and xLSTM. Biological systems lean heavily on recurrence rather than on stacking. We ask how far that shape can go on language modeling. We propose Grounded Prediction Networks (GPN): one state vector revisited at every step through a single recurrent block - one FFN, one shared matrix memory.
