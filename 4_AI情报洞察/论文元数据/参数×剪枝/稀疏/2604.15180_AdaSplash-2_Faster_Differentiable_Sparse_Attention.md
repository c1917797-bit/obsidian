## 摘要

Sparse attention has been proposed as a way to alleviate the quadratic cost of transformers, a central bottleneck in long-context training. A promising line of work is $α$-entmax attention, a differentiable sparse alternative to softmax that enables input-dependent sparsity yet has lagged behind softmax due to the computational overhead necessary to compute the normalizer $τ$. In this paper, we introduce AdaSplash-2, which addresses this limitation through a novel histogram-based initialization that reduces the number of iterations needed to compute $τ$ to typically 1--2.
