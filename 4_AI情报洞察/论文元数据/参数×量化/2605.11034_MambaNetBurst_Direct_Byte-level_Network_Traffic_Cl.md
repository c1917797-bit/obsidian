## 摘要

We present MambaNetBurst, a compact tokenizer-free byte-level sequence classifier for network burst classification based on a Mamba-2 backbone. In contrast to most recent strong traffic-classification and intrusion-detection approaches, our method operates directly on raw packet bytes, avoids tokenization, patching, and heavy engineered multimodal representations, and does not require any self-supervised pre-training stage. Given a packet flow, we form a fixed-length burst from the first few packets, embed the resulting byte sequence appending a learnable CLS token, and process it with a stack
