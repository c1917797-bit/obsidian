## 摘要

Knowledge distillation plays a key role in compressing the Large Language Models (LLMs), which boosts a small-size student model under large teacher models' guidance. However, existing LLM distillation methods overly rely on student-generated outputs, which may introduce generation errors and misguide the distillation process.  Moreover, the distillation loss functions introduced in previous works struggle to align the most informative part due to the complex distribution of LLMs' outputs. To ad
