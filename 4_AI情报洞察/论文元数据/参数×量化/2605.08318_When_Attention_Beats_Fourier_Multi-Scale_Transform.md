## 摘要

We study the problem of \emph{architecture selection} for deep learning models trained to solve partial differential equations (PDEs), asking when transformer-based architectures with learned attention outperform Fourier-domain neural operators. We introduce the \textbf{Multi-Scale Attention Transformer} (\msat{}), a deep learning architecture that encodes spatiotemporal solution histories as token sequences and trains end-to-end via a composite supervised objective with optional physics-informed regularization terms.
