## 摘要

We present a systematic study of fine-tuning OpenAI's Whisper large-v3 for Swiss German ASR, using 1,367 hours of broadcast speech paired with Standard German subtitles as weak supervision. Through 16 iterative training runs on an NVIDIA DGX Spark (Grace Blackwell, 128 GB unified memory, up to 1 PFLOP FP4), we compare LoRA and full fine-tuning of the 1.55B-parameter model, investigate hallucination root causes, and quantify the effect of data quality, subtitle alignment, and training strategy. Our best model achieves 25.6% measured WER on the All Swiss German Dialects Test Set (ASGDTS) in an h
