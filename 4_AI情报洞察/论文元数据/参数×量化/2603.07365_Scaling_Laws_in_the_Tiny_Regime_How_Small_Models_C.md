## 摘要

Neural scaling laws describe how model performance improves as a power law with size, but existing work focuses on models above 100M parameters. The sub-20M regime -- where TinyML and edge AI operate -- remains unexamined. We train 90 models (22K--19.8M parameters) across two architectures (plain ConvNet, MobileNetV2) on CIFAR-100, varying width while holding depth and training fixed. Both follow approximate power laws in error rate: $α= 0.156 \pm 0.002$ (ScaleCNN) and $α= 0.106 \pm 0.001$ (MobileNetV2) across five seeds.
