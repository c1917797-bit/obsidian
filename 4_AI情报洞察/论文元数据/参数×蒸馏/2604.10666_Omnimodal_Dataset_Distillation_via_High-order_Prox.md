Dataset distillation compresses large-scale datasets into compact synthetic sets while preserving training performance, but existing methods are largely restricted to single-modal or bimodal settings. Extending dataset distillation to scenarios involving more than two modalities, i.e., Omnimodal Dataset Distillation, remains underexplored and challenging due to increased heterogeneity and complex cross-modal interactions.
