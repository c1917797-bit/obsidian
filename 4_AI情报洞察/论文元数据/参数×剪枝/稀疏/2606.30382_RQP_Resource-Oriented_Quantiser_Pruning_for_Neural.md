## 摘要

High granularity quantisation (HGQ) exploits weight-level quantisation and pruning to design resource-efficient neural network accelerators, achieving an attractive trade-off between accuracy and hardware utilisation. HGQ is particularly well suited to FPGA-based edge neural network applications. Standard HGQ workflow starts from a high-precision model and progressively reduces bit width, guided by gradient-based optimisation to outline the Pareto frontier. This monotonic and irreversible pruning process is computationally intensive and can overlook the optimal subnetwork for a given resource
