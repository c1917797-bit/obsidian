## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) exhibit severe multilingual safety misalignment: they possess strong safeguards in high-resource languages but remain highly vulnerable to jailbreak attacks in low-resource languages. Current safety alignment methods generally rely on high-quality response data for each target language, which is expensive and difficult to generate. In this paper, we propose a cross-lingual safeguard transfer framework named Multilingual Self-Distillation (MSD).
