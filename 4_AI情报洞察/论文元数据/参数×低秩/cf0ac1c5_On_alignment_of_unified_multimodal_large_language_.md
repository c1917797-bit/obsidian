## 摘要

Unified Multi-Modal Large Language Models (U-MLLMs) have demonstrated strong capabilities in text-to-image (T2I) generation, but most post-training methods still rely on sparse, image-level rewards and place limited emphasis on safety. In this work, we take an exploratory view of \emph{dense} reward signals for U-MLLMs: token-level feedback derived from existing reward and evaluation models. Rather than proposing a new RL algorithm, We study how dense rewards can be extracted, how they behave, and how they can be integrated into the standard Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) framework.
