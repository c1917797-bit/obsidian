## 摘要

Grammar-constrained generation is often combined with local vocabulary masking and speculative decoding, but the resulting sampling law is not the grammar-conditional distribution users usually intend. We show that any speculative decoder with local mask access, Leviathan rejection, and rollback soundness samples from the locally projected distribution $μ^{\mathrm{proj}}$ rather than the grammar-conditional distribution $μ^\star$.
