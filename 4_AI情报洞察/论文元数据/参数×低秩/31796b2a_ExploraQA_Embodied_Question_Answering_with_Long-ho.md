## 摘要

Embodied Question Answering (EQA) is a critical task for developing embodied intelligence, requiring agents to autonomously explore environments and answer human questions through perception, navigation, and reasoning. However, existing EQA benchmarks suffer from three key limitations: constrained exploration scope, passive trajectory, and insufficient viewpoint annotation. To address these challenges, we introduce ExploraQA, a large-scale dataset featuring 12,436 diverse, open-ended questions across seven categories, designed to evaluate language, visual, and spatial reasoning.
