## 摘要

Size transfer methods in Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) are a common treatment to mitigate the high cost of training on large graphs, by transferring the model trained on randomly sampled smaller graphs. However, the theoretical guarantee of such transfer has only been proved in previous studies for random graphs with restricted sparsity. In practice, downsampled real-world graphs may exhibit multiple sparsity regimes. To fully understand the theoretical performance across arbitrary sparsity, we establish the GCN transferability bound by introducing Stretched Graphon Convolutional Network
