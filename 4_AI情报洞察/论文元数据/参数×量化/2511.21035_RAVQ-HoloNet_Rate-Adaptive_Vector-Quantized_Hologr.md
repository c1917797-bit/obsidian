## 摘要

Holography offers significant potential for AR/VR applications. However, its adoption is limited by the high demand for data compression. Existing deep learning approaches generally lack rate adaptivity within a single network and often require multiple models to cover different bandwidth requirements. We present RAVQ-HoloNet, a rate-adaptive vector quantization framework that integrates the rate-adaptive compression with the transformation of image data into phase-only hologram.
