## 摘要

We identify the Spectral Energy Gain in extreme model compression, where low-rank binary approximations outperform tiny-rank floating-point baselines for heavy-tailed spectra. However, prior attempts fail to realize this potential, trailing state-of-the-art 1-bit methods. We attribute this degradation to Latent Geometry Misalignment: standard singular vectors exhibit high coherence (spiky distribution), the worst-case geometry for binary quantization.
