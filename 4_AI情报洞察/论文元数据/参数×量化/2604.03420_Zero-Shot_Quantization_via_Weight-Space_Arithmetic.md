## 摘要

We show that robustness to post-training quantization (PTQ) is a transferable direction in weight space. We call this direction the quantization vector: extracted from a donor task by simple weight-space arithmetic, it can be used to patch a receiver model and improve post-PTQ Top-1 accuracy by up to 60 points in a 3-bit setting, without receiver-side quantization-aware training (QAT). Because the method requires no receiver training data, it provides a zero-shot, low-cost alternative to QAT for extremely low-bit deployment.
