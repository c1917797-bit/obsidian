## 摘要

Selecting the optimal student architecture remains an overlooked challenge in knowledge distillation (KD). Current approaches typically rely on model size constraints or random selection, ignoring how student architecture and inductive biases impact distillation effectiveness. We formulate this as an unsupervised model selection problem, where the goal is to select the best student for a given teacher without requiring ground-truth labels or expensive training cycles.
