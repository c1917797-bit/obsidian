## 摘要

The rapid growth of LLMs demands high-throughput, memory-capacity-intensive inference on resource-constrained edge devices, where single-batch decoding remains fundamentally memory-bound. Existing out-of-core GPU-based and SSD-like accelerators are limited by DRAM-bound weight movement and inefficient storage access granularity. We present NVLLM, a 3D NAND-centric inference architecture that offloads feed-forward network (FFN) computation into the Flash while executing attention on lightweight CMOS logic with external DRAM.
