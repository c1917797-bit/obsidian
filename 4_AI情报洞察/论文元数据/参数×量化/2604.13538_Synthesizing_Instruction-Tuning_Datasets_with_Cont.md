## 摘要

Using responses generated by high-performing large language models (LLMs) for instruction tuning has become a widely adopted approach. However, the existing literature overlooks a property of LLM-generated responses: they conflate world knowledge acquired during pre-training with instruction-following capabilities acquired during post-training. We hypothesize that disentangling the instruction-following capabilities from pre-trained knowledge improves the effectiveness of instruction tuning.
