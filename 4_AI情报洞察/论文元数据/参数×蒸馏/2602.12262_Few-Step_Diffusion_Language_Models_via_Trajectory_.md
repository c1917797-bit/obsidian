## 摘要

Diffusion large language models (DLLMs) have emerged as powerful generative models with the promise of fast text generation through parallel decoding. However, realizing this potential in practice remains challenging: reducing the number of decoding steps, typically causes a substantial degradation in output quality due to token factorization error. To alleviate this, we propose a self-distillation framework that trains a few-step student to match the generative trajectory of a full-step teacher.
