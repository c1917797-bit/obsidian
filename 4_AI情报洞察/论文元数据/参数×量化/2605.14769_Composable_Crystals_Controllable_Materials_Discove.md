## 摘要

De novo crystal generation, a central task in materials discovery, aims to generate crystals that are simultaneously valid, stable, unique, and novel. Existing methods mainly rely on black-box stochastic sampling, providing limited control over how generated structures move beyond the observed distribution. In this paper, we introduce a concept-based compositional framework for crystal generation. We train a vector-quantized variational autoencoder to automatically discover a shared set of reusable crystal concepts, which serve as building blocks for guided generation.
