## 摘要

Scaling context length is reshaping large-model development, yet full-attention Transformers suffer from prohibitive computation and inference bottlenecks at long sequences. A key challenge is to design foundation models that maintain performance and long-context efficiency with minimal training overhead. We introduce SpikingBrain2.0 (SpB2.0), a 5B model that advances both architecture and training efficiency of its predecessor.   Our contributions are two-fold. (1) Architectural Innovation: We propose Dual-Space Sparse Attention (DSSA), an inter-layer hybrid of Sparse Softmax Attention (MoBA)
