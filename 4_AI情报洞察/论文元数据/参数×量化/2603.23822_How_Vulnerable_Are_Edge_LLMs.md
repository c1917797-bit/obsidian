## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed on edge devices under strict computation and quantization constraints, yet their security implications remain unclear. We study query-based knowledge extraction from quantized edge-deployed LLMs under realistic query budgets and show that, although quantization introduces noise, it does not remove the underlying semantic knowledge, allowing substantial behavioral recovery through carefully designed queries.
