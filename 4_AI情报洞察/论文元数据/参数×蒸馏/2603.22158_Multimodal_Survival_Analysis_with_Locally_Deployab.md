## 摘要

We study multimodal survival analysis integrating clinical text, tabular covariates, and genomic profiles using locally deployable large language models (LLMs). As many institutions face tight computational and privacy constraints, this setting motivates the use of lightweight, on-premises models. Our approach jointly estimates calibrated survival probabilities and generates concise, evidence-grounded prognosis text via teacher-student distillation and principled multimodal fusion.
