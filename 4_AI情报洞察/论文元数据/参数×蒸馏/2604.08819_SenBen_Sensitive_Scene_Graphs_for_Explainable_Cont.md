## 摘要

Content moderation systems classify images as safe or unsafe but lack spatial grounding and interpretability: they cannot explain what sensitive behavior was detected, who is involved, or where it occurs. We introduce the Sensitive Benchmark (SenBen), the first large-scale scene graph benchmark for sensitive content, comprising 13,999 frames from 157 movies annotated with Visual Genome-style scene graphs (25 object classes, 28 attributes including affective states such as pain, fear, aggression, and distress, 14 predicates) and 16 sensitivity tags across 5 categories.
