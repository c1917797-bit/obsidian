## 摘要

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for adapting large-scale pre-trained models to downstream tasks with minimal additional parameters. Among PEFT methods, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) stands out for its effectiveness by inserting trainable low-rank matrices into weight updates to enable efficient adaptation. However, when applied to multi-head self-attention, existing LoRA-based methods typically fine-tune each attention head independently, overlooking potential interactions and shared structure among heads.
