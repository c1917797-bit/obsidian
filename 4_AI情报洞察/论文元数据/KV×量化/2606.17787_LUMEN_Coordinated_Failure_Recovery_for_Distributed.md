## 摘要

Modern large language model (LLM) serving clusters distribute inference requests across multiple worker processes on different GPUs, but failures are prevalent at scale. When a worker fails, the cluster simultaneously loses the failed worker's GPU-resident key-value (KV) caches and serving capacity, leaving surviving workers to absorb the redirected traffic while re-running interrupted requests from scratch.
