## 摘要

ReduNet has emerged as a promising white-box neural architecture grounded in the principle of maximal coding rate reduction, offering interpretability in deep feature learning. However, its practical applicability is hindered by computational complexity and limited ability to exploit class-specific structures, especially in undersampled regimes. In this work, we propose Multi-ReduNet and its variant Multi-ReduNet-LastNorm, which decompose the global learning objective into class-wise subproblems.
