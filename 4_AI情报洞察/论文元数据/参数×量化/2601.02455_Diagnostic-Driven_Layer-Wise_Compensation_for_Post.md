## 摘要

Deploying Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) models on memory-constrained edge devices requires aggressive low-bit weight quantization. Layer-wise post-training quantization is practical and effective, but it suffers from cross-layer error accumulation. Existing compensation methods typically use a single global strength for all layers, which is ill-suited to encoder-decoder ASR models whose acoustic encoder and linguistic decoder exhibit markedly different sensitivities to quantization noise.
