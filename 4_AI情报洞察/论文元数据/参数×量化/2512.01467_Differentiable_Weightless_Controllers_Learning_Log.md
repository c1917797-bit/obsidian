## 摘要

Controlling autonomous systems under real-world conditions often requires policies that can be evaluated with low latency and minimal energy consumption. Unfortunately, these conditions are at odds with the use of high-precision deep neural networks as controllers. In this work, we introduce Differentiable Weightless Controllers (DWCs), a symbolic-differentiable architecture that learns flexible, non-linear, yet highly efficient control policies. DWCs can be trained end-to-end via gradient-based techniques, yet compile directly into FPGA-compatible circuits with few- or even single-clock-cycle
