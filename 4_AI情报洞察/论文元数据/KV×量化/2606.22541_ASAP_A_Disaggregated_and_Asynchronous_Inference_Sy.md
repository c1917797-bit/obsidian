## 摘要

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models have become the de facto standard for scaling large language models. To maintain computational efficiency, modern MoE serving systems typically employ a hybrid parallelism strategy, combining Data Parallelism (DP) for attention stages with Expert Parallelism (EP) for MoE stages. However, this design necessitates frequent global synchronization barriers between attention DP groups and experts.
