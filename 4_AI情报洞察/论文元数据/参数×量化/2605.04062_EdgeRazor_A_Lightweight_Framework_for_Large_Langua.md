## 摘要

Quantization has emerged as a mainstream approach for deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) on resource-constrained devices, yet compressing precision below 4-bit typically causes severe performance degradation or prohibitive retraining costs. In this paper, we propose EdgeRazor, a lightweight framework for LLMs via Mixed-Precision Quantization-Aware Distillation.
