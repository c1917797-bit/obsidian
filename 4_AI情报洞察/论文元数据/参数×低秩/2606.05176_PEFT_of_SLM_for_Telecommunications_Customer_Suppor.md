## 摘要

While large language models (LLMs) show strong performance in natural language understanding and generation, their evaluation and adaptation to domain-specific constraints in telecommunications customer support remain limited. In addition, data sovereignty, regulatory constraints, and the handling of sensitive customer and network information complicate the use of externally hosted foundation models in this domain.   We present a systematic study of parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) applied to Qwen2.5-3B to build a domain-specific conversational assistant.
