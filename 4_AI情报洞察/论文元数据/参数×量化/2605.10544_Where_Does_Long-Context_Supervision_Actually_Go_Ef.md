## 摘要

Long-context adaptation is often viewed as window scaling, but this misses a token-level supervision mismatch: in packed training with document masking, each target token's effective context remains short. We introduce EXACT, a supervision-allocation objective that assigns extra weight to long effective-context targets by inverse frequency within the long tail. Across seven Qwen/LLaMA CPT configurations, EXACT improves all 28 trained/extrapolated NoLiMa and RULER comparisons.
