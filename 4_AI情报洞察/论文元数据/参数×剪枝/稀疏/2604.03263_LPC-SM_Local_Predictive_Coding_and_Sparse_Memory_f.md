## 摘要

Most current long-context language models still rely on attention to handle both local interaction and long-range state, which leaves relatively little room to test alternative decompositions of sequence modeling. We propose LPC-SM, a hybrid autoregressive architecture that separates local attention, persistent memory, predictive correction, and run-time control within the same block, and we use Orthogonal Novelty Transport (ONT) to govern slow-memory writes.
