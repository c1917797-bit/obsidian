## 摘要

Sparse Attention Vectors (SAVs) have emerged as an excellent training-free alternative to supervised finetuning or low-rank adaptation to improve the performance of Vision Language Models (VLMs). At their heart, SAVs select a few accurate attention heads for a task of interest and use them as classifiers, rather than relying on the model's prediction. In a similar spirit, we find that directly probing the raw activations of the VLM, in the form of scalar values, is sufficient to yield accurate classifiers on diverse visually grounded downstream tasks.
