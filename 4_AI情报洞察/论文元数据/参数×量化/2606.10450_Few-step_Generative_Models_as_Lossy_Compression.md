## 摘要

DiffC provides a principled way to reuse pre-trained diffusion models for lossy compression, but its encoding and decoding procedures remain slow because they require many discretized forward and reverse steps. We study whether few-step generative models -- Rectified Flow, Consistency Trajectory Models (CTM), and MeanFlow -- can be cast as codecs within the same reverse channel coding (RCC) framework. The main challenge is that RCC requires posterior and shared distribution parameters, whereas these models do not explicitly parameterize intermediate conditional distributions. For Rectified Flo
