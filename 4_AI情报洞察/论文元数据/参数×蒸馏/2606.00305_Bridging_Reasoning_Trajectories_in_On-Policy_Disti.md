## 摘要

On-Policy Distillation (OPD) improves large language model reasoning by training a student model on trajectories sampled from its own policy under teacher supervision. Although OPD operates on trajectories, its learning signal remains token-level: it identifies deviations through high-loss tokens and repairs them through local reverse-KL correction. We show that this "trajectory-sampled but token-learned" mechanism cannot reliably bridge student trajectories toward teacher trajectories.
