## 摘要

Generative retrieval (GR) ranks documents by autoregressively generating document identifiers. Because many GR methods rely on trie-constrained beam search, they are vulnerable to early pruning of relevant prefixes under finite-beam decoding. Planning Ahead in Generative Retrieval (PAG) mitigates this failure mode by using simultaneous decoding to compute a document-level look-ahead prior that guides subsequent sequential decoding.
