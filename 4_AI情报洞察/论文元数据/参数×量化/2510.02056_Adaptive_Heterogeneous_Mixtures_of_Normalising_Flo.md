## 摘要

Normalising-flow variational inference (VI) can approximate complex posteriors, yet single-flow models often behave inconsistently across qualitatively different distributions. We propose Adaptive Mixture Flow Variational Inference (AMF-VI), a heterogeneous mixture of complementary flows (MAF, RealNVP, RBIG) trained in two stages: (i) sequential expert training of individual flows, and (ii) adaptive global weight estimation via likelihood-driven updates, without per-sample gating or architectural changes.
