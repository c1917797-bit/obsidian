## 摘要

We propose a resource-efficient framework for compressing large language models through knowledge distillation, combined with guided chain-of-thought reinforcement learning. Using Qwen 3B as the teacher and Qwen 0.5B as the student, we apply knowledge distillation across English Dolly-15k, Spanish Dolly-15k, and code BugNet and PyTorrent datasets, with hyperparameters tuned in the English setting to optimize student performance. Across tasks, the distilled student retains a substantial portion of the teacher's capability while remaining significantly smaller: 70% to 91% in English, up to 95% i
