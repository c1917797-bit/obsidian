## 摘要

Neural operators provide a powerful framework for learning discretization invariant mappings between function spaces, but standard deterministic models do not capture predictive uncertainty. We introduce diffusion last layer (DLL), a modular probabilistic output head for neural operator backbones. DLL represents target fields through an input dependent low rank expansion inspired by the Karhunen-Loéve expansion and learns a conditional diffusion model over the corresponding coefficient space.
