## 摘要

Layer pruning efficiently reduces Large Language Model (LLM) computational costs but often triggers sudden performance collapse. Existing representation-based analyses struggle to explain this mechanism. We propose studying pruning through decision representation. Focusing on multiple-choice tasks, we introduce two metrics, Decision Margin and Option Frequency, and an Iterative Pruning method to analyze layer-wise decision dynamics. Our findings reveal a sharp decision transition that partitions the network into two stages: a Silent Phase, where the model cannot yet predict the correct answer,
