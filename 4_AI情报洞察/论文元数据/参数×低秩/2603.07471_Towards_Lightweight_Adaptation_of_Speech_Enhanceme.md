## 摘要

Recent studies have shown that post-deployment adaptation can improve the robustness of speech enhancement models in unseen noise conditions. However, existing methods often incur prohibitive computational and memory costs, limiting their suitability for on-device deployment. In this work, we investigate model adaptation in realistic settings with dynamic acoustic scene changes and propose a lightweight framework that augments a frozen backbone with low-rank adapters updated via self-supervised training. Experiments on sequential scene evaluations spanning 111 environments across 37 noise type
