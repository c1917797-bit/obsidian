## 摘要

Speech Language Models (SpeechLMs) model tokenized speech to capture both semantic and acoustic information. When neural audio codecs based on Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ) are used as audio tokenizers, they produce multiple discrete tokens per time step, yielding inherently multi-level representations. To process these multi-level tokens together, prior work typically adopts hierarchical architectures to capture this structure.
