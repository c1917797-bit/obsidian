## 摘要

Large language models have achieved strong reasoning capabilities, though often at the cost of massive parameter counts and expensive inference. In this work, we explore a different direction: adaptive reasoning depth in compact language models. We present CosmicFish-HRM, a compact language model built around a Hierarchical Reasoning Module (HRM) that dynamically allocates computational effort during inference.
