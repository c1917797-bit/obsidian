## 摘要

Efficient mixed-precision MMA operations are critical for accelerating deep learning workloads on GPGPUs. However, existing open-source Tensor Core implementations rely on discrete arithmetic unit designs, leading to high latency, accumulated rounding errors, and poor resource utilization. To address these challenges, we propose Ten-Four, a configurable mixed-precision fused dot product unit integrating both floating-point and integer arithmetic pipelines within a unified architecture, implemented as part of the open-source RISC-V-based Vortex GPGPU's Tensor Core Unit extension.
