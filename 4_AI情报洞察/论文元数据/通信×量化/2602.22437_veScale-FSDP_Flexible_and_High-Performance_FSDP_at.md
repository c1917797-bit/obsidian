## 摘要

Fully Sharded Data Parallel (FSDP), also known as Zero Redundancy Optimizer (ZeRO), is widely used for large-scale model training, because of its memory efficiency and minimal intrusion on model code. However, existing FSDP systems rely on fixed element-wise or row-wise sharding formats that conflict with block-structured computations. As a result, they struggle to support modern structure-aware training methods, including block-wise quantization and non-element-wise optimizers such as Shampoo and Muon.
