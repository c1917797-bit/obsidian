## 摘要

We present SuperKMeans: a k-means variant designed for clustering collections of high-dimensional vector embeddings. SuperKMeans' clustering is up to 7x faster than FAISS and Scikit-Learn on modern CPUs and up to 4x faster than cuVS on GPUs (Figure 1), while maintaining the quality of the resulting centroids for vector similarity search tasks.
