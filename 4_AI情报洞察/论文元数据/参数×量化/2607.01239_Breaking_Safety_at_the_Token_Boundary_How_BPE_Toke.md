## 摘要

Character-level perturbations bypass safety alignment in modern LLMs despite leaving prompts human-readable. We identify and test a central structural mechanism: BPE tokenization fragments safety-critical words into sub-word pieces, and the three public alignment datasets we surveyed contain no intentionally fragmented inputs. The mechanism is a chain, tested end-to-end on five model families (Qwen-3-4B, Qwen-2.5-7B, Gemma-3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B). An optimization targeting safety-token fragmentation flips the first-token refusal trigger on 80-100% of refused HarmBench prompts, with 48%
