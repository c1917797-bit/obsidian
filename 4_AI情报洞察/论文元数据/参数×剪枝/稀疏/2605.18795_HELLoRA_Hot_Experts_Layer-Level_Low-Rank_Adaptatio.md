Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models scale parameters at near-constant per-token compute, and their sparse activation patterns create untapped opportunities for more efficient adaptation. We propose Hot-Experts Layer-level Low-Rank Adaptation (HELLoRA), which attaches LoRA modules only to the most frequently activated experts at each layer.
