## 摘要

Polymer simulations are among the most computationally demanding workloads in soft-matter research, often requiring days of execution and high energy consumption to achieve physically meaningful results. In this work, we address these challenges through the coupling and optimization of two complementary simulation frameworks: the Uneyama-Doi Model (UDM) and the SOft coarse-grained Monte Carlo Acceleration (SOMA). UDM efficiently propagates concentration fields at the continuum level, while SOMA resolves chain-scale thermal fluctuations via particle-based Monte Carlo dynamics. Each model was individually optimized for GPU execution using kernel fusion, memory coalescing, asynchronous random-number generation yielding up to 70% (UDM) and 80% (SOMA) performance improvement. The coupling is performed through our proposed coordinator library that orchestrates data exchange and synchronizes time-stepping across multiple GPUs.
