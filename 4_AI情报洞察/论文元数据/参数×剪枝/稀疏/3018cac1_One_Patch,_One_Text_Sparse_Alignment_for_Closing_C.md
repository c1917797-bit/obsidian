Compositional Zero-Shot Learning (CZSL) aims to recognize unseen attribute-object compositions with learned primitives ($e.g.$, attribute and object) knowledge from seen compositions. Previous methods achieve remarkable results by leveraging powerful cross-modal alignment capabilities of CLIP.
