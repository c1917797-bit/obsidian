## 摘要

The increasing size and complexity of large language models (LLMs) have raised significant challenges in deployment efficiency, particularly under resource constraints. Post-training quantization (PTQ) has emerged as a practical solution by compressing models without requiring retraining. While existing methods focus on uniform quantization schemes for both weights and activations, they often suffer from substantial accuracy degradation due to the non-uniform nature of activation distributions.
