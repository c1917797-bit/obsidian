## 摘要

Large pre-trained language models are increasingly adapted to downstream tasks using parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), but existing PEFT methods are typically deterministic and unimodal, making them poorly suited for low-resource multimodal settings where predictive uncertainty and cross-modal reliability both matter. We introduce CALIBER (Context-Aware Low-rank Inference with Bayesian Embedding Regularization), a multimodal uncertainty-aware PEFT framework for audio-text learning. CALIBER extends Bayesian low-rank adaptation by conditioning the variational posterior in the adapter space
