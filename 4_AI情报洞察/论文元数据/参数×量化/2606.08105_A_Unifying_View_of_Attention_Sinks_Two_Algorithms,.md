## 摘要

When attention concentrates on a single token, a sink, what is the model actually computing? Attention sinks are ubiquitous in softmax transformers, yet this shared visual signature can hide fundamentally different algorithms. We show that visually similar sink patterns can reflect two distinct mechanisms: {i} adaptive nop, where a head suppresses its update by routing to a null token, and {ii} broadcast, where a sink aggregates and redistributes global information.
