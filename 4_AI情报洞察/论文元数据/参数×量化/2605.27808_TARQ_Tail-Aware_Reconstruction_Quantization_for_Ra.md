## 摘要

Data-aware post-training quantization (PTQ) minimizes a per-token reconstruction loss on a small calibration corpus, implicitly weighting positions by their empirical frequency. For \textbf{A}utomatic \textbf{S}peech \textbf{R}ecognition (ASR), this misaligns with tail-sensitive risk: names, numerals, and domain-specific words receive proportionally little calibration mass. We propose \textbf{Tail-Aware Reconstruction Quantization} (\TARQ), a label-free PTQ framework that shifts calibration toward the lexical tail via \textbf{\rareBAL}, a closed-form per-Linear-layer rule equalizing common/tai
