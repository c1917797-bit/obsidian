## 摘要

Large Language Models (LLMs) frequently memorize sensitive training data thereby creating significant privacy and copyright risks. Addressing these risks, i.e., removing such knowledge from an existing model checkpoint, has proven challenging as many unlearning methods lead to catastrophic utility loss or are ineffective for complex queries. We introduce Divergence Decoding (DD), a mechanism that uses small auxiliary models to steer the logits of the LLM away from specific data during inference. Training these models is straight forward, i.e., we use standard pre-training and fine-tuning setup
