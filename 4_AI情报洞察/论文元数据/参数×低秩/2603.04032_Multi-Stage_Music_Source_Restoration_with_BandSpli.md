## 摘要

Music Source Restoration (MSR) targets recovery of original, unprocessed instrument stems from fully mixed and mastered audio, where production effects and distribution artifacts violate common linear-mixture assumptions. This technical report presents the CP-JKU team's system for the MSR ICASSP Challenge 2025. Our approach decomposes MSR into separation and restoration. First, a single BandSplit-RoFormer separator predicts eight stems plus an auxiliary other stem, and is trained with a three-stage curriculum that progresses from 4-stem warm-start fine-tuning (with LoRA) to 8-stem extension vi
