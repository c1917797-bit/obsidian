## 摘要

In-Context Learning and Chain-of-Thought prompting improve reasoning in large language models (LLMs). These typically come at the cost of longer, more expensive prompts that may contain redundant information. Prompt compression based on pruning offers a practical solution, yet existing methods rely on sequential token removal which is computationally intensive. We present DiffuMask, a diffusion-based framework integrating hierarchical shot-level and token-level pruning signals, that enables rapid and parallel prompt pruning via iterative mask prediction.
