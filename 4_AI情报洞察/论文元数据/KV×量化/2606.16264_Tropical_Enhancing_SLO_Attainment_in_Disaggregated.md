## 摘要

To guarantee service quality in transformer based large language model (LLM) serving, it is essential to meet the latency constraints of both the prefill phase (measured by Time-to-First-Token, TTFT) and the decode phase (measured by Time-per-Output-Token, TPOT). Non-disaggregated serving places prefill and decode on the same worker, while disaggregated serving places the prefill and decode on isolated workers.
