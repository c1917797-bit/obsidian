## 摘要

Log parsing is a fundamental step in automated log analysis, transforming raw system logs into structured event templates for downstream tasks such as anomaly detection and system monitoring. Existing log parsing methods range from rule-based and clustering-based approaches to neural models that learn semantic representations from log messages. However, neural approaches typically rely on dense matrix multiplications, which can result in high computational cost and energy consumption.
