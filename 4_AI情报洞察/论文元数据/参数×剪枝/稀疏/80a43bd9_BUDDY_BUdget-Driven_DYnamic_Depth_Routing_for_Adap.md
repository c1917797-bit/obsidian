## 摘要

Large language models require substantial computational resources for inference due to their massive number of parameters. Model layer pruning accelerates inference by eliminating redundant layers. However, existing layer pruning methods fail to meet users' flexible budget constraints and lack the ability to adaptively adjust the inference path. To address these issues, we propose Buddy, a budget-driven and adaptive inference framework. Specifically, we design a Decision Module that adaptively selects important layers to execute based on user input while satisfying a given budget constraint. A
