## 摘要

Multi-observation deblurring seeks to recover a clear image from multiple blurred observations, yet most methods assume centralized access to full-scene data, which is unrealistic in practical scenarios where images are captured by distributed, independent devices. We introduce a more realistic federated setting where each client holds a private, partially overlapping view of a larger scene. This creates an “information jigsaw puzzle” that must be solved under strict privacy and regulatory constraints, without sharing raw images, kernels, or intermediate image estimates.
