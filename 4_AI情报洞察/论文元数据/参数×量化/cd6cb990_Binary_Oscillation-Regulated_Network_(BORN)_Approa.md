## 摘要

Binary Neural Networks (BNNs) are gaining attention for making energy-intensive deep learning more accessible to resource-constrained edge devices. Traditionally, training methods for such models focus on minimizing quantization error in forward propagation and approximating the $sign$ function for gradient computation. However, these methods overlook the BNN-specific phenomenon of oscillations and do not adapt the learning rate to the training dynamics.
