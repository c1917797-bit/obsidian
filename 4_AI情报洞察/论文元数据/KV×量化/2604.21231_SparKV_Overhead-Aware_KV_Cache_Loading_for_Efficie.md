## 摘要

Efficient inference for on-device Large Language Models (LLMs) remains challenging due to limited hardware resources and the high cost of the prefill stage, which processes the full input context to construct Key-Value (KV) caches. We present SparKV, an adaptive KV loading framework that combines cloud-based KV streaming with on-device computation. SparKV models the cost of individual KV chunks and decides whether each chunk should be streamed or computed locally, while overlapping the two execution paths to reduce latency.
