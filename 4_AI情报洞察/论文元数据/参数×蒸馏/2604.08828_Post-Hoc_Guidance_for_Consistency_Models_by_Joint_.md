## 摘要

Classifier-free Guidance (CFG) lets practitioners trade-off fidelity against diversity in Diffusion Models (DMs). The practicality of CFG is however hindered by DMs sampling cost. On the other hand, Consistency Models (CMs) generate images in one or a few steps, but existing guidance methods require knowledge distillation from a separate DM teacher, limiting CFG to Consistency Distillation (CD) methods. We propose Joint Flow Distribution Learning (JFDL), a lightweight alignment method enabling guidance in a pre-trained CM. With a pre-trained CM as an ordinary differential equation (ODE) solver
