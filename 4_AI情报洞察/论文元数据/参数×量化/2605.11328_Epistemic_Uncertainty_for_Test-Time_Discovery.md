## 摘要

Automated scientific discovery using large language models relies on identifying genuinely novel solutions. Standard reinforcement learning penalizes high-variance mutations, which leads the policy to prioritize familiar patterns. As a result, the maximum reward plateaus even as the average reward increases. Overcoming this limitation requires a signal that distinguishes unexplored regions from intrinsically difficult problems.
