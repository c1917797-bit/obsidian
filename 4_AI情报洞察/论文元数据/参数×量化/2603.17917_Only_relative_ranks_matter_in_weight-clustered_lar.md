## 摘要

Large language models (LLMs) contain billions of parameters, yet many exact values are not essential. We show that what matters most is the relative rank of weights-whether one connection is stronger or weaker than another-rather than precise magnitudes. To reduce the number of unique weight values, we apply weight clustering to pretrained models, replacing every weight matrix with K shared values from K-means.
