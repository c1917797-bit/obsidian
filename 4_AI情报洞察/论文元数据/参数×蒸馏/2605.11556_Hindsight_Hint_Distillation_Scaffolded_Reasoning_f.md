## 摘要

Solving complex long-horizon tasks requires strong planning and reasoning capabilities. Although datasets with explicit chain-of-thought (CoT) rationales can substantially benefit learning, they are costly to obtain. To address this challenge, we propose Hindsight Hint Distillation (HHD), which only requires easy-to-obtain question-answer pairs without CoT annotations. Inspired by how human teachers use student mistakes to provide targeted guidance, HHD synthesizes hindsight hints from the model's own failed self-rollouts and uses them to scaffold on-policy rollouts that successfully complete
