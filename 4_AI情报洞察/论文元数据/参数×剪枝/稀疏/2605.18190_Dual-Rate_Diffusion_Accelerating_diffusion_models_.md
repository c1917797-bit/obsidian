## 摘要

Diffusion models achieve state-of-the-art generative performance but suffer from high computational costs during inference due to the repeated evaluation of a heavy neural network. In this work, we propose Dual-Rate Diffusion, a method to accelerate sampling by interleaving the execution of a heavy high-capacity context encoder and a light efficient denoising model. The context encoder is evaluated sparsely to extract high-dimensional features, which are effectively reused by the light denoising model at every step to refine the sample efficiently.
