## 摘要

Instruction tuning of large vision-language models (LVLMs) increasingly depends on massive multimodal corpora, yet these datasets contain samples with substantial redundancy, low visual dependency, and highly imbalanced coverage of multimodal reasoning behaviors. As a result, uniform subsampling or naive score-based selection often yields suboptimal training subsets. We introduce MAGIC, a training-free, forward-only coreset selection method designed to construct compact yet behaviorally faithful subsets for multimodal instruction tuning.
