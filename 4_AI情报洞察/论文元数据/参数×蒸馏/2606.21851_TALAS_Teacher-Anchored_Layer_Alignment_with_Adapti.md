Knowledge Distillation (KD) has established itself as a pivotal technique for compressing large pre-trained language models. However, existing methods that force a student to strictly mimic the teacher's sentence embeddings or internal features often incur prohibitive computational costs and yield suboptimal performance due to the inherent capacity gap.
