## 摘要

We present LoRA-MINT, a new methodology for Membership Inference Test (MINT) applied to recent Large Language Models (LLMs) fine-tuned for specific Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks through Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA). The primary goal is to assess whether individual samples were part of the training data of these adapted models, providing a useful auditing tool for the management of intellectual property and sensitive data. Our analysis explores the relationship between model perplexity and membership status, providing a systematic framework for estimating data exposure in fine-tuned LLM
