## 摘要

Standard Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models rely on centralized routing mechanisms that introduce rigid inductive biases. We propose Routing-Free MoE which eliminates any hard-coded centralized designs including external routers, Softmax, Top-K and load balancing, instead encapsulating all activation functionalities within individual experts and directly optimized through continuous gradient flow, enabling each expert to determine its activation entirely on its own. We introduce a unified adaptive load-balancing framework to simultaneously optimize both expert-balancing and token-balancing object
