Over-the-air computation (AirComp) has traditionally been built on the principle of pre-embedding computation into transmitted waveforms or on exploiting massive antenna arrays, often requiring the wireless multiple-access channel (MAC) to operate under conditions that approximate an ideal computational medium. This paper introduces a new computation framework, termed out-of-air computation (AirCPU), which establishes a joint source-channel coding foundation in which computation is not embedded before transmission but is instead extracted from the wireless superposition by exploiting structure
