## 摘要

Embedding models are crucial to modern NLP. However, the creation of the most effective models relies on carefully constructed supervised finetuning data. For high resource languages, such as English, such datasets are readily available. However, for hundreds of other languages, they are simply non-existent. We investigate whether the advent of large language models can help to bridge this gap. We test three different strategies for generating synthetic triplet data used to optimise embedding models.
