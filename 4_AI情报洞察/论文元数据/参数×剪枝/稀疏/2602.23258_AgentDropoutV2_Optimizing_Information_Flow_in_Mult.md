## 摘要

While Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) excel in complex reasoning, they suffer from the cascading impact of erroneous information from individual agents. Current solutions often resort to rigid structural engineering or expensive fine-tuning, limiting their adaptability. We propose AgentDropoutV2 (ADv2), a test-time rectify-or-reject pruning framework that dynamically optimizes MAS information flow.
