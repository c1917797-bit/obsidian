## 摘要

Scaled Outer Product (SOP) is a post-training quantization methodology for large language model weights, designed to deliver near-lossless fidelity at 4.5--6 bits per weight on hardware with per-layer LUT decode. The methodology combines per-layer search of fixed and dynamic codebook pairs selected by a per-block selection bit, signed per-block scales, activation-weighted cosine selection, and multiple-choice knapsack promotion of sensitive layers with outlier and sparse-residual correction.
