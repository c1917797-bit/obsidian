## 摘要

Post training quantization is essential for deploying large language models (LLMs) on resource constrained hardware, yet state of the art methods enforce uniform bit widths across layers, yielding suboptimal accuracy efficiency trade offs. We present RAMP (Reinforcement Adaptive Mixed Precision), an off policy Soft Actor Critic framework that learns per layer bit width assignments to minimize perplexity under a global bit budget. The policy conditions on an 11 dimensional embedding of activation statistics, weight properties, and structural descriptors, enabling zero shot transfer across model
