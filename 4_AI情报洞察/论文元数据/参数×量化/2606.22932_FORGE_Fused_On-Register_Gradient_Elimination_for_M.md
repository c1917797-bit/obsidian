## 摘要

Reverse-mode differentiation computes every weight gradient, writes it to memory, and only then lets the optimizer read it back. This two-phase schedule sets the memory ceiling of modern training: at the seam between the phases, every layer's gradient is live at once. We argue that this materialized gradient is an artifact of how differentiation is staged, not a quantity that learning requires -- and we eliminate it.
