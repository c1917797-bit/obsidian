## 摘要

Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning (HRL) demonstrates highly efficient exploration in long-horizon decision-making problems with sparse rewards via the Semi-Markov Decision Process (SMDP). However, we observe a structural limitation of SMDP in HRL: once calling a subtask, the agent is locked into a fixed course of action, losing the flexibility to adapt to other higher-value subtasks, which is a critical barrier in optimality.
