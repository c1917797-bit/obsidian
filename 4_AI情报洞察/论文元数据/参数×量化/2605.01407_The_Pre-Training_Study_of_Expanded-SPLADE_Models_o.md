## 摘要

Masked Language Modeling (MLM) pre-training is one of the primary ways to initialize Neural Information Retrieval (IR) models prior to retrieval fine-tuning. However, studies show that MLM pre-trained models have limited readiness and transfer learning issues for fine-tuning them into Neural Bi-Encoder models. This paper studies the effect of different pre-training datasets and pre-training options on the MLM pre-trained models for retrieval fine-tuning. The study focuses on the SPLADE-style model, which uses the MLM layer also at fine-tuning time. More specifically, we experimented with Expan
