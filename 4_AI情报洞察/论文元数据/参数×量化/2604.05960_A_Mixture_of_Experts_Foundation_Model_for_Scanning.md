## 摘要

Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) is indispensable in modern materials science, enabling high-resolution imaging across a wide range of structural, chemical, and functional investigations. However, SEM imaging remains constrained by task-specific models and labor-intensive acquisition processes that limit its scalability across diverse applications. Here, we introduce the first foundation model for SEM images, pretrained on a large corpus of multi-instrument, multi-condition scientific micrographs, enabling generalization across diverse material systems and imaging conditions.
