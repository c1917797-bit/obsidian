## 摘要

Digital health research has advanced dynamic graph-based disease models, topological learning on simplicial complexes, and multimodal mixture-of-experts architectures, but these strands remain largely disconnected. We propose Graph Vector Field (GVF), a framework that models health risk as a vector-valued field on time-varying simplicial complexes, coupling discrete differential-geometric operators with modality-structured mixture-of-experts.
