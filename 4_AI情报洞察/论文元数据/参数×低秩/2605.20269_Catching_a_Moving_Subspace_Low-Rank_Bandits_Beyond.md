## 摘要

Many bandit deployments (recommendation, clinical dosing, ad targeting) share two facts prior work handles only in isolation: rewards live on a low-dimensional latent subspace, and that subspace drifts. Stationary low-rank bandits exploit rank but break under subspace change; non-stationary linear bandits adapt to drift but pay ambient rate $\widetilde{O}(d\sqrt{T})$. We study piecewise-stationary low-rank linear contextual bandits with scalar feedback: $θ_t = B_k^\star w_t$ with rank-$r$ factor $B_k^\star\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times r}$ constant within each of $K$ unknown segments and able to shift
