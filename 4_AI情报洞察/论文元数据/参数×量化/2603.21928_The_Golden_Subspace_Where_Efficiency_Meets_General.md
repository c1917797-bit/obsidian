## 摘要

Continual Test-Time Adaptation (CTTA) aims to enable models to adapt online to unlabeled data streams under distribution shift without accessing source data. Existing CTTA methods face an efficiency-generalization trade-off: updating more parameters improves adaptation but severely reduces online inference efficiency. An ideal solution is to achieve comparable adaptation with minimal feature updates; we call this minimal subspace the golden subspace.
