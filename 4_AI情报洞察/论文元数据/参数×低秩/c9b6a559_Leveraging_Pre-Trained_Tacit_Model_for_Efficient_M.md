## 摘要

Exploration inefficiency caused by large policy spaces is a common challenge in multi-agent reinforcement learning. Although incorporating prior knowledge has been demonstrated to improve exploration efficiency, existing methods typically model it as intrinsic rewards, which may violate potential-based conditions, leading to policy deviation and hindering optimal policy learning. To address this, we propose a novel two-phase multi-agent learning framework, **PTMC** (**P**re-training **T**acit **M**odel for efficient **C**oordination), comprising pre-training and coordinated training phases.
