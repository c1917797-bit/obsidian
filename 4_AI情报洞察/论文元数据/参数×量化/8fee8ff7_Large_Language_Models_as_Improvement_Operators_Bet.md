## 摘要

Reasoning training of  LLMs teaches them to produce long chains of thought (long CoT), with attendant increase in accuracy (good!), context length (bad!), compute cost (bad!) and  answer latency (bad!). Can current models provide other combinations on this Pareto frontier, e.g., give answers with better accuracy than long CoT models, despite lower context length and/or latency? We show that the answer is ``yes'' and also give ways to systematically think about these design choices.
