## 摘要

Recent Speech-to-Speech Translation (S2ST) systems achieve strong semantic accuracy yet consistently strip away non-verbal vocalizations (NVs), such as laughter and crying that convey pragmatic intent, which severely limits real-world utility. We address this via three contributions. First, we propose a synthesis pipeline for building scalable expressive datasets to overcome the data scarcity limitation.
