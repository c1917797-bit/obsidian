## 摘要

Knowledge distillation is widely used to improve generalization in practice, yet its theoretical understanding remains elusive. In the standard distillation setting, a teacher model provides soft predictions to guide the training of a student model. We model teacher and student training as coupled stochastic processes and introduce a distillation divergence, defined as the Kullback-Leibler divergence between these two stochastic kernels.
