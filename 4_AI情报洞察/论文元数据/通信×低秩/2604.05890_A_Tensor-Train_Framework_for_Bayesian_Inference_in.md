## 摘要

Bayesian inference in high-dimensional discrete-input additive noise models is a fundamental challenge in communication systems, as the support of the required joint a posteriori probability (APP) mass function grows exponentially with the number of unknown variables. In this work, we propose a tensor-train (TT) framework for tractable, near-optimal Bayesian inference in discrete-input additive noise models. The central insight is that the joint log-APP mass function admits an exact low-rank representation in the TT format, enabling compact storage and efficient computations. To recover symbol
