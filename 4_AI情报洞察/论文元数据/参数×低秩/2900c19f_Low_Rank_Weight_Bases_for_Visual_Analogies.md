## 摘要

Visual analogy learning enables image manipulation through demonstration rather than textual description, allowing users to specify complex transformations that are difficult to articulate in words. Given a triplet $\\{\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{a}', \mathbf{b}\\}$, the goal is to generate $\mathbf{b}'$ such that $\mathbf{a} : \mathbf{a}' :: \mathbf{b} : \mathbf{b}'$. Recent methods adapt text-to-image models to the analogy task using a single Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) module, but they face a fundamental limitation: attempting to capture the diverse space of visual transformations within a fixed ada
